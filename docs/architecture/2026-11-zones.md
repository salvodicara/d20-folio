# Zones: persistent areas (2026-11-03)

Edge-case family M9 ([edge cases](2026-11-edge-cases.md)). The rules session owns the catalogue
(`SrdSpellData.zone`: anchor, spell level, light, spares, triggers; #614). This lane owns the
instances and what a moment triggers.

## The instance

A zone is a persistent combat effect on the encounter, with the payload `{ kind: "zone",
spellId, spared? }`, in the same append-only `effectOps` ledger as every other standing effect.

- **No rules change.** That channel is already written by members and DMs, so the security rules
  stay as they are.
- **Its fields.** The actor is the caster, `source.castLevel` the level it was cast at, and the
  duration its source's: Concentration (revoked when the caster drops it, by the existing shell
  bridge), or the encounter.
- **One per caster and spell.** Casting it again replaces it (the stacking key).
- **Older clients.** A v0.25 client drops an unknown payload at its tolerant read.

## The runtime (`lib/zones`)

- `activeZones(effects)` lists the encounter's zones, with the spell's `zone` and the cast level.
- `zoneTriggers(zone, moment, ledger)` says whether a moment fires the zone on a creature.
  - The moments are enter, start of turn, end of turn, moving within, and the zone moved onto it.
  - Each spell triggers only on the moments it lists. It spares the creatures the caster chose,
    and counts forced movement.
  - A zone hits a creature at most once per turn (the ledger: zone and creature → turn).
    Moving within (Spike Growth, per 5 feet) has no such limit.
- `zoneLight(zones)` is the light where zones overlap: darkness and light dispel each other by
  level (Daylight beats a 2nd-level Darkness; a 4th-level Darkness beats Daylight), and on a tie
  the newer zone wins.

There is no geometry: the table says who enters or stands where; the engine says whether it counts.
