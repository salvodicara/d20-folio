# Edge-case corpus (D&D 2024)

Collected 2026-11-02 from dnd2024.wikidot.com, the 2024 Sage Advice Compendium, the 2025 errata summaries, Foundry dnd5e issues, D&D Beyond and EN World threads. Paraphrased, one source per case. The verdicts against the engine are in [verdicts.md](verdicts.md); the families and the plan are in [../2026-11-edge-cases.md](../2026-11-edge-cases.md).

## 1. Stacking and ordering

- **Same-name effects don't stack** — Two castings of the same spell, or two copies of the same feature, never add together. Only the strongest copy applies, or the newest one if they are equally strong. The engine needs effect identity (by name/source), not just a list of modifiers. [GLOSS]
- **Different names do stack** — Bardic Inspiration and Guidance both add a die to the same check because their names differ. "Don't stack" is checked by name, not by "both are dice bonuses". [SAC #48]
- **Item bonuses stack, but not duplicate attunement** — Ring of Protection plus Bracers of Defense both add to AC. You can't attune to two copies of the same item, so a second Ring of Protection gives nothing. [SAC #159]
- **Advantage/disadvantage cancel regardless of count** — Three sources of advantage and one of disadvantage still give a straight roll. Neither side stacks, so the engine must collect boolean sources, not count them. [GLOSS]
- **Lucky now grants advantage, not a third die** — Lucky spends a point to give the roll advantage, or to give an attacker disadvantage, so it goes through the normal cancellation rule. Under 2014 it rolled an extra die that ignored cancellation. (2024 change) [WIKI feat:lucky http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:lucky]
- **Damage modifier order** — Flat adjustments and multipliers come first, then Resistance (halve), then Vulnerability (double). Each applies only once per damage instance, however many sources grant it. So a resisted breath weapon on a successful save is halved twice: once for the save, once for Resistance. [SAC #36; GLOSS]
- **Damage floors at 0** — Resistance or a flat reduction can bring damage down to 0 but never below, so damage can't turn into healing. [SAC #35]
- **Arcane Ward's place in the pipeline** — Resistance is applied first, then the Ward absorbs, then temp HP, then real HP. Damage absorbers have a defined slot in the order. [SAC #73]
- **Temp HP never add together** — A new grant of temporary HP doesn't add to the old one: the creature keeps whichever pool it chooses. Heroism refreshing every turn doesn't build up a bigger pool. Unless the source says otherwise, temp HP last until they are used up or a Long Rest ends. [GLOSS; SAC #126]
- **Temp HP extend effects tied to them** — Armor of Agathys ends when you have no temp HP left, but it counts temp HP from any source. Stacking it with Polymorph, False Life or Wild Shape keeps the retaliation going. The engine needs a "has any temp HP" state, not pools tagged by origin. (2024 change) [SAC #112; KIBBLES #19; FVTT#3247]
- **Only one AC formula at a time** — Unarmored Defense (Barbarian or Monk), Mage Armor and Draconic Resilience are alternative base formulas. You use one, never a sum. [WIKI class:multiclassing http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/class:multiclassing; SAC #129]
- **AC formula vs AC bonus** — Mage Armor works with a shield, but Monk Unarmored Defense doesn't. Shield, Shield of Faith, cover and Bladesong add on top of whatever formula is active. The engine must separate "base formula" from "additive bonus". [SAC #128]
- **Barkskin is a floor, not a formula** — Barkskin raises AC to 17 if it is lower, ignoring smaller modifiers, but a higher AC from other sources still wins. That is a third kind of AC effect, alongside formulas and bonuses. (2024 change) [SAC #113]
- **Overlapping paladin auras** — A creature inside several Aura of Protection emanations gets the bonus from only one, and the creature chooses which. That is a third stacking policy beside "strongest" and "newest". [WIKI paladin:main http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/paladin:main]
- **Simultaneous effects** — When several things trigger at once, whoever's turn it is chooses the order. The engine can't hard-code a fixed order. [GLOSS]
- **Exhaustion is the only condition that stacks** — Every other condition is either on or off. Exhaustion has levels, and two separate sources must add up. Foundry got this wrong: its effects didn't add levels. [GLOSS; FVTT#7618]
- **Same-name speed reductions** — Several Slow-mastery hits don't reduce speed below the 10 ft maximum. A Slow hit, Ray of Frost and Exhaustion are different effects, so they do add up. [KIBBLES #30; WIKI fighter:main http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/fighter:main]
- **Extra Attack doesn't stack across classes** — Extra Attack from two classes still gives two attacks. Thirsting Blade adds nothing if you already have Extra Attack. [WIKI class:multiclassing]

## 2. Concentration

- **A new concentration spell ends the old one** — Starting another effect that needs concentration ends the current one at once. Casting a concentration spell as a ritual still needs concentration while you cast it. [GLOSS; SAC #104]
- **Damage-save DC uses damage before temp HP** — The save is Constitution, DC 10 or half the damage (whichever is higher), capped at 30. Damage soaked by temp HP still counts, so a 30-damage hit fully absorbed by temp HP still calls for DC 15. Foundry once skipped the prompt in this case. [SAC #109; FVTT#5394]
- **One save per damage instance** — Each separate damage source calls for its own save. Multi-hit effects (rays, darts, beams, multiattacks) can force several saves in one turn. Whether Magic Missile's darts striking together count as one instance or several is (disputed). [GLOSS; SAC #97]
- **Incapacitated or dead ends concentration** — Being Stunned, Paralysed, Unconscious or knocked out by Cunning Strike drops concentration. Being Prone or Grappled doesn't. [GLOSS]
- **Rage forbids concentration** — Entering Rage ends your current concentration, and you can't cast spells while raging. Spells that don't need concentration keep running. [WIKI barbarian:main http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/barbarian:main; SAC (2014 carry-over) on Spiritual Weapon]
- **Shape-shifting doesn't end concentration** — A Druid in Wild Shape, or a polymorphed caster, keeps concentrating even though it can't cast new spells. The save uses the form's Constitution, plus your proficiency if that is better. A Moon Druid at level 6+ also adds Wisdom. [WIKI druid:main http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/druid:main; SAC #134; WIKI druid:moon http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/druid:moon]
- **Advantage and disadvantage on the save** — War Caster and Eldritch Mind give advantage, and Extended Spell does too (2024 change). Mage Slayer's damage imposes disadvantage, and the two cancel. [WIKI feat:war-caster http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:war-caster; WIKI feat:mage-slayer http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:mage-slayer; https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/]
- **No range or sight needed** — Concentration continues however far you move from the effect and whether or not you can see it, unless the spell says otherwise. [SAC #108]
- **Readied spells need concentration** — Readying a spell spends the slot right away and needs concentration until the trigger. That ends any other concentration you hold, and if the trigger never comes, the slot is lost. [SAC #26; GLOSS]
- **Concentration-linked summons** — When concentration on Summon Beast or Find Steed-like spells ends, the creature disappears and drops what it was carrying. Foundry still can't tie summoned actors to concentration (open feature request). [WIKI spell:summon-beast http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:summon-beast; FVTT#3543]
- **Several effects, one concentration** — One spell can apply several effects to many targets, and they must all end together. Foundry links only one activity, or one effect per actor, to a concentration. [FVTT#5282; FVTT#5637]
- **What survives when concentration ends** — Instantaneous results (damage, healing, creations) stay. Ongoing effects stop. Some spells become permanent once held for their full duration (True Polymorph). [WIKI spell:true-polymorph http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:true-polymorph]
- **Ending an effect can trigger a penalty** — Some effects cause something when they end (a falling flyer, a creature reverting, a spell with an after-effect). The engine needs end-of-effect triggers, not just removal. [GLOSS (Shape-Shifting)]
- **Concentration moves become new spells (2024 change)** — Spiritual Weapon now needs concentration, while Divine Favor and Magic Weapon no longer do. Concentration is per-spell data, so the 2014 and 2024 versions of a spell differ. [https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/spiritual-weapon-nerf; KIBBLES #38]
- **Hex and Hunter's Mark can be moved** — When the cursed target drops to 0 HP, a later Bonus Action moves the same concentration effect to a new target. A higher slot only lengthens the duration. [WIKI spell:hex http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:hex]
- **Wild Shape ends on Incapacitated, but concentration is separate** — If the Druid is Stunned, Wild Shape ends and concentration also ends. These are two effects ending from one trigger. [WIKI druid:main]

## 3. Forms and transformations

- **Wild Shape 2024: your HP, plus temp HP** — You keep your own HP and Hit Point Dice and gain temp HP equal to your Druid level. There is no separate beast HP pool anymore. (2024 change) [WIKI druid:main; https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1755-the-2024-circle-of-the-moon-druid-and-changes-to]
- **Moon Druid temp HP** — A Moon Druid gains temp HP equal to three times its Druid level. Temp HP don't add together, so this replaces the base grant or any existing temp HP (you choose the larger pool). AC becomes 13 + Wisdom modifier if that beats the beast's AC. [WIKI druid:moon]
- **What Wild Shape keeps** — You keep creature type, HP, Hit Point Dice, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma, class features, languages and feats. Skill and save proficiencies use the better of yours and the beast's. Species and background traits are not kept (a narrower list than 2014). Foundry offers only all-or-nothing for features. (2024 change) [WIKI druid:main; FVTT#6638]
- **Temp HP when leaving Wild Shape** — Nothing in the rules says Wild Shape temp HP vanish when you revert, so they may persist until a Long Rest. Tools disagree. (disputed) [FVTT#5962]
- **Wild Shape at 0 HP** — Because you use your own HP, dropping to 0 knocks you Unconscious. That makes you Incapacitated, which ends the form. There is no 2014-style spill-over to a second pool. Players still expect the old behaviour. (2024 change) (disputed) [FVTT#833; https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/druid/225982-the-2024-druid/next]
- **No spellcasting in beast form, with exceptions** — A Druid can't cast in Wild Shape until Beast Spells at level 18, and even then not spells with costly or consumed materials. Moon Druid circle spells can be cast in form. The ability to cast depends on form, feature and spell together. [WIKI druid:main; WIKI druid:moon]
- **Equipment in Wild Shape** — Per item, gear falls, merges (and has no effect) or stays worn (the DM decides whether it fits). Foundry still counts the beast's encumbrance. [WIKI druid:main; FVTT#5738]
- **Polymorph 2024** — The target's statistics become the beast's. It keeps its own HP and Hit Point Dice, gains temp HP equal to the beast's HP, and the spell ends when those temp HP run out. Leftover temp HP vanish when the spell ends (errata). (2024 change) [WIKI spell:polymorph http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:polymorph; ERRATA]
- **Polymorph and other effects** — Spells already on the creature stay on it through the transformation. Damage left over after reverting is checked against the true form's resistances. A willing target also takes on the beast's mental scores. [SAC #135; SAC #136; SAC #137]
- **True Polymorph permanence vs temp HP** — After a full hour of concentration it lasts until dispelled. But the temp HP vanish when the spell ends and, under the general rule, also at a Long Rest. Players argue whether the permanent form survives its first Long Rest. (disputed) [WIKI spell:true-polymorph; KIBBLES #31]
- **True Polymorph: object into creature** — The new creature must be no larger than the object and CR 9 or lower. It is Friendly, acts right after you and obeys you. After an hour you lose control of it. One transformation creates a controlled actor and changes allegiance later. [WIKI spell:true-polymorph]
- **Shapechange** — You keep your Spellcasting feature, mental scores, HP and proficiencies, and a Magic action changes form. Temp HP come only from the first form (errata closed a loophole that refilled them). Whether you get the new form's legendary actions is (disputed). [WIKI spell:shapechange http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:shapechange; ERRATA; https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/rules-game-mechanics/215397-legendary-actions-in-mm-2024/previous]
- **Animal Shapes refresh loophole** — Changing form again used to grant a fresh temp HP pool. After the errata, the pool equals the first form's HP and a Bonus Action ends the transformation. (2024 change) [ERRATA]
- **Magic Jar swaps the body, not the mind** — You use the host's HP, Hit Point Dice, Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, speed and senses, and keep everything else. If the host dies, a Charisma save decides whether you return to the jar or die. Destroying the jar sends souls home, or kills them if their body is too far away. [WIKI spell:magic-jar http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:magic-jar]
- **Ongoing effects carry over by default** — Shape-shifting doesn't end effects on the creature unless their text says so, and a creature reverts to its true form on death. [GLOSS (Shape-Shifting)]
- **Size change can free you from restraints** — Changing size frees you from a grapple or restraint only if you stop meeting that effect's size limit. [SAC #52; https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/sage-advice-returns]
- **Monster shape-changers keep some stats** — For example, a Deva keeps its mental scores and Constitution while gaining a new form's Strength and Dexterity and its legendary features. Foundry can't express partial stat retention. [FVTT#7153]
- **Truesight reveals transformations** — A creature with Truesight perceives the true form, which is a per-observer view of a transformed creature. [GLOSS]

## 4. Summons and companions

- **2024 summon stat blocks are slot-parameterised** — Summon Beast's AC is 11 + spell level and its HP is a base plus 5 per level above 2. Its attack damage includes the spell level, and it makes half the spell level in attacks (rounded down). The stat block is a template evaluated at cast time. [WIKI spell:summon-beast]
- **Summons share initiative but act after you** — The summon acts on your initiative count, immediately after your turn. It obeys verbal commands that cost you no action; with no command it Dodges and avoids danger. [WIKI spell:summon-beast; https://www.aidedd.org/spell/summon-beast]
- **Conjure spells are zones now, not creatures** — Conjure Animals summons an intangible pack that moves with you. It forces a Dexterity save when it comes within 10 ft of a creature, or when a creature enters or ends its turn there, at most once per turn per creature. The engine must model it as a moving area effect. (2024 change) [WIKI spell:conjure-animals http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:conjure-animals]
- **Conjure Minor Elementals is a per-attack buff** — It adds dice to every attack's damage, including each Scorching Ray ray. The errata cut its scaling from 2d8 to 1d8 per slot level above 4. (2024 change) [https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/conjure-minor-elementals-nerf; ERRATA; KIBBLES #25]
- **Find Familiar** — The familiar rolls its own initiative and acts on its own turn. It can't attack, but it can Help, and it counts as your ally for Sneak Attack. It delivers your touch spells with its Reaction. Recasting changes its form. At 0 HP it vanishes and drops its gear. [WIKI spell:find-familiar http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:find-familiar; SAC #123; SAC #124; FVTT#4009]
- **Druid Wild Companion** — Spending a slot or a Wild Shape use gives a Fey familiar that lasts until a Long Rest. One summon can be paid for with two different resources. [WIKI druid:main]
- **Find Steed** — The steed's AC, HP and Hit Dice scale with spell level, and it gains a Fly speed at 4th level+. It shares your initiative and acts on its own if you're Incapacitated. Its Life Bond heals it whenever your spells heal you. It vanishes at 0 HP or if you die. [WIKI spell:find-steed http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:find-steed]
- **Beast Master companion** — The beast acts on your turn and Dodges unless commanded. A Bonus Action commands it, or you trade one of your Attack-action attacks for its Strike. If you're Incapacitated it acts freely. Its HP scale with Ranger level, so they must be recomputed on level-up. [WIKI ranger:beast-master http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/ranger:beast-master]
- **Beast Master revive and replace** — Within an hour of its death, a Magic action and a spell slot bring the beast back with full HP after 1 minute. After a Long Rest you may summon a different beast, and the old one vanishes. [WIKI ranger:beast-master; https://rpgbot.net/dnd5/characters/classes/2024-ranger/ranger-subclasses/]
- **Beast Master scaling features** — At level 11 Beast's Strike happens twice, and its first hit each turn on your Hunter's Mark target adds that spell's damage. At level 15, spells you cast on yourself also affect the beast within 30 ft. Companion behaviour depends on the owner's active spells. [WIKI ranger:beast-master]
- **True Polymorph's controlled creature** — It acts right after you and obeys you for an hour, after which control ends (see section 3). [WIKI spell:true-polymorph]
- **Antimagic and summons** — Magically summoned creatures wink out inside an Antimagic Field and return when it ends. Foundry's antimagic doesn't handle them. [FVTT#7684]
- **Unseen Servant isn't a combatant** — It is neither ally nor enemy, so it gets no initiative entry. [SAC #145]
- **Summon spell attacks without saves** — The Giant Insect spirit's web attack can set speed to 0 with no save and no size limit, across a multiattack. Summon actions carry conditions that the engine must apply. [KIBBLES #20]
- **Putrid Spirit paralysis** — The summoned spirit can paralyse an already-poisoned creature on a hit with no save. Summon riders depend on the target's current conditions. [KIBBLES #16]
- **Summon placement and range** — Range limits where a summon appears, and Foundry's summon activity doesn't enforce a maximum range. [FVTT#6032]
- **One familiar at a time** — Casting the spell again changes the existing familiar's form instead of adding a second one. [WIKI spell:find-familiar]

## 5. Multiclass and spellcasting

- **Shared slot table** — Add full-caster levels, half of Paladin and Ranger levels (rounded up) and a third of Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster levels (rounded down), then look up the multiclass table. The combined slots can cast any class's prepared spells, at a higher level if you like. [WIKI class:multiclassing]
- **Pact Magic is a separate pool** — Warlock slots are all of one level and come back on a Short Rest. They can cast prepared spells from other classes, and normal slots can cast Warlock spells, so the two pools must be separate but interchangeable. [WIKI class:multiclassing; FVTT#6862]
- **Prepared spells belong to one class** — Each class prepares its own list as if you were single-classed, and each prepared spell uses that class's casting ability. The same spell prepared through two classes gives two DCs. [WIKI class:multiclassing]
- **Cantrips scale by character level** — Cantrip damage and Eldritch Blast beam count use total character level, not class level. You get every cantrip granted by every class. [WIKI class:multiclassing; SAC #41]
- **Multiclass prerequisites use base scores** — You need 13 in the relevant abilities, using base scores without temporary boosts. You gain only some of the new class's proficiencies and never its saving throws. [SAC #40; SAC #42]
- **One spell with a slot per turn** — On any turn you may spend only one spell slot on a spell, whether it is an action, Bonus Action or Reaction spell. A cantrip alongside is fine. The limit counts slots, not spells. (2024 change) [https://dungeonmister.com/guides/one-spell-slot-per-turn/; SAC #102; SAC #103]
- **Slotless casts escape the limit** — Spell scrolls, Mystic Arcanum, free once-per-rest casts from feats or features, and item casts don't spend slots, so they can be cast alongside a slotted spell. (disputed: a BG3 mod applies the limit to scrolls) [KIBBLES #21; https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/23218]
- **Quickened Spell doesn't give two slotted spells** — It turns an action spell into a Bonus Action, but the one-slot rule still applies. [SAC #65]
- **Font of Magic slots vanish** — Sorcery Points convert to slots of 5th level at most, with minimum Sorcerer levels. Created slots disappear at a Long Rest, which ends the 2014 "coffeelock" banking trick. (2024 change) [WIKI sorcerer:main http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/sorcerer:main]
- **Spells from feats and species** — A Magic Initiate spell can be cast once per Long Rest for free, or with your slots, using the ability chosen with the feat. You can choose your own class's list. Each spell needs its own record of source, ability, free uses and slot use. [SAC #85; ITEMS]
- **Ritual casting differs by class** — A Wizard can ritual-cast any ritual in its spellbook without preparing it. Other classes must have the spell prepared. A ritual cast uses no slot, so it doesn't restore an Abjurer's Arcane Ward. [SAC #76; SAC #72; SAC #93]
- **Spells cast without slots use base level** — Unless stated, they are cast at their lowest level. Monster versions may name an equivalent level. [SAC #101]
- **Items that cast spells** — They use the lowest level, need no components and require concentration if the spell does. The DC is 8 + spellcasting modifier + proficiency, with a +0 modifier if you have no casting ability. [ITEMS; SAC #155]
- **Spell Scroll class restriction** — A scroll can be used only if its spell is on your class's list (a Bard can use spells it gained through Magical Secrets), even though anyone can read a general scroll. [SAC #50; SAC #158; ITEMS]
- **Wild Magic in multiclass casters** — Only spells prepared through Sorcerer features trigger Wild Magic Surges, so the engine must track which class a spell was cast through. [SAC #45]
- **Nature Magician converts uses into slots** — At level 20, unused Wild Shape uses become one spell slot (2 levels per use), once per Long Rest. One resource becomes another. [WIKI druid:main]
- **Spell attacks aren't weapon attacks** — A spell attack can't replace an attack in the Attack action or Extra Attack, or be an Opportunity Attack. True Strike can't be used with Extra Attack, but its attack can trigger Sneak Attack. [SAC #98; SAC #99; SAC #144]

## 6. Reactions and timing

- **One reaction per round** — A creature regains its Reaction at the start of its own turn. It can use it on its own turn, but a reaction spell on your turn still counts against the one-slot rule. [SAC #103; GLOSS]
- **Counterspell 2024** — It triggers on seeing a creature within 60 ft cast a spell with V, S or M components. The caster makes a Constitution save instead of the old ability check. On a failure the spell does nothing, the action is wasted, and the slot is not spent. Counterspell itself is Somatic only. (2024 change) [WIKI spell:counterspell http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:counterspell]
- **Subtle Spell vs Counterspell** — Subtle removes V and S components and only free, unconsumed M components. A spell left with a costly material can still be seen and countered. (disputed) [https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/dungeons-dragons-discussion/rules-game-mechanics/232094-subtle-spell-and-circle-magic?comment=47]
- **Counterspell vs slotless monster casts** — Most 2024 monsters cast "X/day" instead of with slots, so "the slot isn't spent" doesn't apply. Whether a countered daily use is lost is (disputed). [https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/rules-game-mechanics/218392-magic-casting/next; ERRATA (1/Day Each)]
- **Shield changes the past** — It is cast after an attack hits (or when Magic Missile targets you). The +5 AC applies to the triggering attack and lasts until the start of your next turn. The engine must re-check a hit that has already been resolved. [WIKI spell:shield http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:shield]
- **"When hit" vs "when targeted" vs "when damaged"** — Shield works on a hit or a Magic Missile targeting. Uncanny Dodge and Deflect Attacks work on a hit, before damage is applied. Hellish Rebuke works after damage. Uncanny Dodge never works against saving-throw effects. [SAC #59; WIKI monk:main http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/monk:main; WIKI rogue:main http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/rogue:main]
- **Readied action timing** — The trigger must be perceivable. The readied action happens right after the trigger, such as between a monster's multiattack swings. The target must be in range when it triggers, and you may instead take a different reaction such as an Opportunity Attack. You can't delay your turn. [SAC #13; SAC #14; SAC #25; SAC #27]
- **Opportunity Attacks** — An OA triggers when a creature you can see leaves your reach using its own movement. Forced movement and teleportation don't trigger it. A reach weapon uses its own reach, and you can't make one against a creature you can't see unless you have Blindsight. [SAC #28; SAC #29; GLOSS]
- **OA as Grapple or Shove** — The OA can be an Unarmed Strike's Grapple or Shove, which allows "opportunity shoves" on allies. In 2024 the trigger isn't limited to hostile creatures. (2024 change) [SAC #30; KIBBLES #3]
- **War Caster spell OA** — Instead of an OA you cast a one-action spell that targets only the leaving creature. Whether that can be a buff on an ally who walks away is (disputed). [WIKI feat:war-caster; https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/war-caster-debate; KIBBLES #4]
- **Sentinel** — An adjacent creature that Disengages or attacks someone else provokes an OA. A hit sets its speed to 0 for the turn, and the Sentinel attack resolves after its trigger. [WIKI feat:sentinel http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:sentinel]
- **Riposte and misses** — Riposte can answer a missed Opportunity Attack. Grapple and Shove use no attack roll, so they can't trigger it. [SAC #32; SAC #53]
- **Post-roll reactions** — Cutting Words reduces any damage roll, even auto-hit Magic Missile. Bardic Inspiration is used after a d20 Test fails. Heroic Inspiration rerolls any die right after it is rolled. Each opens a different decision window: before the roll, after the roll but before the result, after the result. (2024 change) [SAC #49; https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/]
- **Choose-to-succeed effects** — Mage Slayer's Guarded Mind, Legendary Resistance and Stroke of Luck turn a failed result into a success or a 20, which is a fourth window: after the outcome is known. [WIKI feat:mage-slayer; WIKI rogue:main]
- **Sneak Attack on others' turns** — It is limited once per turn, not per round, so a reaction OA on an enemy's turn can deal a second Sneak Attack. [SAC #58]
- **Legendary actions** — They happen right after another creature's turn. Each costs one use (no more multi-cost actions), uses return at the start of the monster's turn, and the monster gets an extra use in its lair. (2024 change) [https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1890-preview-the-new-stat-block-design-in-the-2024]
- **Alert initiative swap** — Right after initiative is rolled, you can swap results with a willing ally, unless either of you is Incapacitated. The initiative order changes after it has been computed. [WIKI feat:alert http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:alert]
- **Ready and Charger** — Charger's attack needs movement and an attack on the same turn, so a readied action can't use it. [SAC #24]

## 7. Effects that modify other effects

- **One Metamagic per spell, with exceptions** — Normally one option per spell. Sorcery Incarnate allows two while Innate Sorcery is active, and Arcane Apotheosis makes one free per turn. Each option can still be used only once per spell. [WIKI sorcerer:main; SAC #62]
- **Twinned Spell 2024** — It works only on spells that gain an extra target when upcast, and it counts as raising the spell's level by 1. The level check happens after the boost (a level-7 Sorcerer twins a 4th-level Banishment as 5th). It can apply to a spell copied by Wish. (2024 change) [https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1769-2024-sorcerer-vs-2014-sorcerer-whats-new; SAC #66]
- **Careful and Heightened Spell** — Careful's protected creatures take no damage on a successful save (2024 change). Heightened gives disadvantage on every save one target makes against the spell, for its whole duration. [https://dungeonmister.com/guides/classes-in-dungeons-dragons/sorcerer-metamagic-dnd-5e-2024/]
- **Empowered Spell across rays** — You can reroll damage dice from several Scorching Ray rays, but no more dice in total than the limit. The limit applies to the whole spell, not to each damage roll. [SAC #64]
- **Elemental Affinity and Empowered Evocation** — The bonus applies to one damage roll per spell, even when there are several rays. [SAC #63]
- **Elemental Adept** — Your spells of the chosen type ignore Resistance (not Immunity), and you treat 1s on their damage dice as 2s. The engine must handle "ignore resistance" for one attacker only, and a per-die minimum. [WIKI feat:elemental-adept http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:elemental-adept]
- **Great Weapon Fighting is weapon dice only** — Its minimum of 3 per die doesn't apply to Divine Smite or Hex dice added to the same hit. Savage Attacker also rerolls only the weapon's dice. Damage dice must stay tagged by source. [SAC #84; SAC #87; https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/sage-advice-returns]
- **Critical hits double every damage die** — All dice tied to the attack are rolled twice: weapon, Sneak Attack, Divine Smite, Hex. Flat bonuses are not doubled. A Valor Bard's inspiration die added to damage counts as a damage die. [GLOSS; SAC #51; WIKI spell:divine-smite http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:divine-smite]
- **Negative dice on a crit** — Ray of Enfeeblement subtracts 1d8 from damage. Doubling dice on a crit would subtract 2d8, which is probably wrong. Effects that subtract dice must be excluded from crit doubling. (disputed) [FVTT#7047]
- **Non-damage riders aren't doubled** — A Shadow's Strength drain on a crit isn't damage, so it isn't doubled. [SAC #151]
- **Reliable Talent sets a minimum on the die** — A d20 of 9 or less on a proficient skill check counts as 10, before modifiers. It doesn't combine with Jack of All Trades, but it can combine with Remarkable Athlete. [WIKI rogue:main; SAC #61]
- **Tactical Mind** — Adds 1d10 to a failed check, and the Second Wind use is spent only if the check then succeeds. The cost depends on the outcome. [WIKI fighter:main]
- **Indomitable and Disciplined Survivor** — Each rerolls a failed save: Indomitable adds the Fighter level, and you must use the new roll. Rerolls and adders stack on the same save. [WIKI fighter:main; WIKI monk:main]
- **Innate Sorcery** — For 1 minute it gives +1 to your Sorcerer spell DC and advantage on your Sorcerer spell attacks. The DC depends on which class the spell came from. [WIKI sorcerer:main]
- **Cunning Strike costs dice before the roll** — You remove Sneak Attack dice before rolling to buy effects (Poison, Trip, Withdraw; Daze, Knock Out, Obscure later). Two effects are allowed from level 11. The damage roll changes before it happens. [WIKI rogue:main]
- **Stunning Strike on a success** — Even on a successful save the target's speed is halved and the next attack against it has advantage. A successful save still has an effect. (2024 change) [WIKI monk:main; KIBBLES #10]
- **Shillelagh and other die swaps** — Shillelagh changes your weapon's die, but not the d4 bonus attack from Polearm Master. That attack still adds your ability modifier. [SAC #140; SAC #86]
- **Bless and Bane** — They add or subtract a d4 on attack rolls and saves. They stack with other differently named dice (see section 1). [SAC #48]

## 8. Resource economy and rests

- **Long Rest restores all Hit Point Dice** — In 2024 a Long Rest gives back every spent Hit Die, where 2014 gave back half. (2024 change) [GLOSS]
- **Interrupted rests** — A Long Rest is broken by initiative, casting anything other than a cantrip, taking damage, or an hour of exertion. If you rested at least an hour, you still get Short Rest benefits, and resuming adds an hour per interruption. A Short Rest needs at least 1 HP. [GLOSS]
- **Short Rests: the only limit is time** — There is no cap on Short Rests per day; story and interruptions limit them. Casting a spell during a Short Rest interrupts it. [SAC #7; GLOSS]
- **Partial Short Rest recovery** — Second Wind, Channel Divinity and Wild Shape regain one use on a Short Rest and all uses on a Long Rest. A recovery rule can be "N on Short, all on Long". [WIKI fighter:main; WIKI druid:main]
- **Features that refresh on initiative** — Persistent Rage gets back all Rage uses (once per Long Rest). Uncanny Metabolism restores Focus and heals (once per Long Rest). Perfect Focus tops Focus up to 4 if you didn't use Metabolism. Evergreen Wild Shape gives one use if you have none. The trigger is "initiative rolled", and the features have their own conditions. [WIKI barbarian:main; WIKI monk:main; WIKI druid:main]
- **Once-per-rest uses of a recovery feature** — Sorcerous Restoration gets back up to half your Sorcerer level in points on a Short Rest, once per Long Rest. Usage limits nest inside each other. [WIKI sorcerer:main]
- **Once per turn vs once on each of your turns** — "Once per turn" (Sneak Attack, Stunning Strike, Primal Strike, Lunar Form) can happen again on enemy turns. "Your turn" and "first attack on your turn" (Reckless Attack) can't. [SAC #58; WIKI druid:moon; WIKI barbarian:main]
- **Per-turn save limits on areas** — Spirit Guardians and Conjure Animals force a save at most once per turn per creature, but every turn counts. Pushing an enemy through the area on an ally's turn means it can take damage twice per round. [https://www.enworld.org/threads/spirt-guardians.706656/; https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/divine-lawnmower-2024-rules; KIBBLES #17; KIBBLES #18]
- **Rage duration upkeep** — Rage lasts until the end of your next turn and is extended by attacking, forcing a save or using a Bonus Action to extend it, up to 10 minutes. Heavy armour or being Incapacitated ends it. From level 15 it lasts the full 10 minutes with no upkeep. (2024 change) [WIKI barbarian:main]
- **Relentless Rage escalating DC** — Each use raises the DC by 5, and a Short or Long Rest resets it to 10. The counter resets on rests. [WIKI barbarian:main]
- **Monster Recharge** — A "Recharge 5–6" ability is rolled at the start of the monster's turn. 2024 monsters list innate spells as "1/Day Each", so every spell has its own counter (errata). [ERRATA]
- **Item charges and dawn** — Charges come back at dawn, or when the DM decides on planes with no dawn. An attuned creature knows the charge count. Some items roll a d20 when the last charge is used and are destroyed on a 1. [ITEMS]
- **Lay on Hands pool** — The pool is 5 × Paladin level. Spending 5 points removes Poisoned, and from level 14 removes Blinded, Charmed and other conditions, each without healing. One pool pays for different effects. [WIKI paladin:main]
- **Free casts plus slot casts** — Paladin's Smite (one free Divine Smite per Long Rest) and Faithful Steed (one free Find Steed) are counters that sit beside slot casting. [WIKI paladin:main]
- **Moonlight Step uses** — Uses equal your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1) per Long Rest, and a 2nd+ level slot buys one more. Some resources can be bought with other resources. [WIKI druid:moon]
- **Exhaustion recovery** — A Long Rest removes one level, and Greater Restoration removes one. The Ranger's Tireless removes one on a Short Rest. [GLOSS; SAC #10; https://arcaneeye.com/mechanic-overview/exhaustion-5e/]
- **Temp HP expire on a Long Rest** — Unless the source gives a duration, temp HP end at a Long Rest. This ties True Polymorph and Wild Shape leftovers to rest processing. [GLOSS]
- **Heroic Inspiration** — You hold at most one. Humans gain it on each Long Rest, and the Champion's Heroic Warrior grants it at the start of each turn if you have none. (2024 change) [https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/]

## 9. Conditions and exhaustion

- **2024 exhaustion** — Each level gives −2 per level to every d20 Test and −5 ft speed per level, and level 6 kills. Initiative (a Dexterity check), concentration saves and death saves are all d20 Tests, so all take the penalty. Spell save DCs don't. (2024 change) [GLOSS; https://arcaneeye.com/mechanic-overview/exhaustion-5e/]
- **Exhaustion immunity** — Foundry doubled exhaustion on creatures immune to it, and broke its 5.x level migration. A levelled condition needs immunity checks and safe data migration. [FVTT#7492; FVTT#7561]
- **Invisible is relative to the observer** — Attacks against you have disadvantage and your attacks have advantage only against creatures that can't see you. You also have advantage on initiative. Every check must ask "can this attacker see that target?" [GLOSS]
- **Hide grants Invisible "while hidden"** — Hiding needs a DC 15 Stealth check. You stay hidden until you're found, make a loud noise, make an attack roll or cast a spell with a Verbal component. The errata made ending Hide remove only the Hide-granted invisibility. (2024 change) [ERRATA; FVTT#7397; https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/52099/roleplaying-games/dd-2024-hiding-invisibility]
- **What counts as "found"** — Whether being found needs a Perception check against your Stealth result, or whether stepping out to attack keeps the advantage, is argued at the table. (disputed) [https://www.enworld.org/threads/reworking-the-2024-hiding-rules.712813/; https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/stealth-invisibility-condition-hide-action-one-dnd-2024]
- **Blindsight and Truesight reveal hidden creatures** — They end the Invisible condition that Hide granted. Hide-invisibility and spell-invisibility are different sources of the same condition. [SAC #4]
- **Incapacitated** — No actions, Bonus Actions or Reactions, concentration ends, you can't speak, and you have disadvantage on initiative. Wild Shape, Rage, Evasion, Danger Sense and Aura of Protection all switch off. One condition flips many features. [GLOSS; WIKI paladin:main; WIKI barbarian:main]
- **Unconscious** — It includes Incapacitated and Prone. You drop what you hold and automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saves. Attacks against you have advantage, and a hit from within 5 ft is a critical. Conditions that include other conditions need expansion. [GLOSS]
- **Stunned can move** — In 2024 Stunned no longer stops movement, which deliberately separates it from Paralysed. (2024 change) [SAC #5]
- **Grappled 2024** — Your speed is 0 and your attacks against anyone except the grappler have disadvantage. The grappler moves at extra cost while dragging you. You escape with an Athletics or Acrobatics check, and the grapple ends if the grappler is Incapacitated. Spells and attacks are still allowed. (2024 change) [GLOSS; SAC #31]
- **Grapple's escape DC** — The target makes a Strength or Dexterity save against 8 + modifier + PB instead of an opposed check, and a grapple needs a free hand per creature. Monks can use Dexterity for the DC. (2024 change) [https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/; WIKI monk:main]
- **Grappled + Prone** — Standing up costs half your speed, and a speed of 0 means you can't stand. Knocking down a grappler doesn't knock down the creature it holds. [GLOSS; SAC #33]
- **Prone ranged vs melee** — Attacks against a prone creature have advantage only from within 5 ft and disadvantage beyond. The prone creature's own attacks have disadvantage. [GLOSS]
- **Frightened needs line of sight** — If you can't see the source of fear, the disadvantage doesn't apply. Heroism suppresses Frightened on its target while it lasts, and the condition comes back when it ends. "Suppressed" is a third state beside present and removed. [SAC #12; SAC #127]
- **A condition doesn't stack with itself, but each source keeps its own duration** — Two Frightened effects from different sources each have their own end condition. Removing one leaves the other. [GLOSS]
- **Rogue Knock Out** — Unconscious for 1 minute or until it takes damage, with a save at the end of each of its turns. The condition has two ending conditions. [WIKI rogue:main]
- **Abjure Foes** — Frightened, and on its turns limited to one of movement, an action or a Bonus Action. It ends on damage. A rider limits the action economy. [WIKI paladin:main]
- **Sanctuary breaking** — It ends only if the warded creature attacks, casts a spell or deals damage. Grapples and shoves don't end it. [SAC #139]

## 10. Death and revival

- **Death saves** — Rolled at the start of your turn while at 0 HP: 10+ is a success and 3 successes stabilise you. A natural 20 brings you back with 1 HP, and a natural 1 counts as two failures. Damage at 0 HP counts as one failure, two if it is a critical. Exhaustion's d20 penalty also applies here. [GLOSS]
- **Instant death** — If damage takes you to 0 and the remaining damage equals or exceeds your HP maximum, you die outright. Temp HP and resistance change the remaining amount, so the order of steps matters. [GLOSS]
- **Temp HP while at 0** — If temp HP gained at 0 absorb all of a hit, it is unclear whether you "took damage" and suffer a failed death save. (disputed) [GLOSS; SAC #109 (analogous for concentration)]
- **Stable creatures** — A stable creature at 0 HP regains 1 HP after 1d4 hours. Healing of any amount at 0 HP wakes you. [GLOSS; https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/]
- **Knocking out** — A melee attack (including a melee spell attack) that would drop a creature to 0 can leave it at 1 HP and Unconscious instead. The attacker decides when the damage is dealt. [GLOSS; SAC #37]
- **Disintegrate and Relentless Endurance** — Disintegrate turns you to dust only if it leaves you at 0 HP. The Orc's Relentless Endurance sets you to 1 HP first, so it prevents the dusting. Death-prevention runs before the "at 0" check. [SAC #117; https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/sage-advice-returns]
- **Relentless Rage** — Dropping to 0 while raging (and not killed outright) calls for a Constitution save. On a success your HP become twice your Barbarian level. Several "drop to 0" savers (Orc trait, Relentless Rage) need an order, which the player chooses. [WIKI barbarian:main]
- **Wild Shape at 0** — You fall Unconscious in your own body and the form ends. With the 2024 HP model there is no buffer to spill damage into. (2024 change) [WIKI druid:main; FVTT#833]
- **Revivify** — Within 1 minute of death, with a 300 GP diamond that is consumed, the target returns with 1 HP. It doesn't work on death from old age or restore missing parts. In 2024 more classes can cast it. [WIKI spell:revivify http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:revivify]
- **Raise Dead penalty** — Within 10 days, the target returns with 1 HP, with poisons neutralised. It has −4 to d20 Tests, reduced by 1 per Long Rest. Two decaying penalties (this and Exhaustion) can apply together. [WIKI spell:raise-dead http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:raise-dead]
- **Dead creatures and conditions** — A revived creature may keep conditions it had. Its spirit must be willing to return, and attunement ends at death (as summarised from the glossary; check the book). (disputed) [GLOSS]
- **Monsters at 0 usually die** — Monsters at 0 HP die unless the DM says otherwise, while PCs make death saves. Different rules apply depending on whether the creature is a player character. [GLOSS]
- **Familiar and steed at 0** — They vanish and leave their gear behind instead of dying. The Beast Master's beast does die and can be revived within an hour. Each companion type handles 0 HP differently. [WIKI spell:find-familiar; WIKI spell:find-steed; WIKI ranger:beast-master]
- **Magic Jar death** — If the host dies, a Charisma save decides between returning to the jar and dying. If your body is dead or out of range when the spell ends, you die. [WIKI spell:magic-jar]
- **Polymorph at 0 temp HP** — The spell ends when the temp HP run out, and extra damage goes to your real HP after your true form's resistances. There is no extra pool like the 2014 version had. (2024 change) [WIKI spell:polymorph; SAC #136]
- **Lingering penalties from Resurrection-type spells** — The higher-level revival spells have their own penalties and time limits. Each needs a timer that decays on Long Rests. [WIKI spell:raise-dead]

## 11. Movement and mounts

- **Speed changes cover every speed** — An effect that changes or zeroes speed applies to every speed (walk, climb, fly, swim) for the same duration. When switching speeds, the distance already moved is subtracted. [GLOSS]
- **Difficult terrain doesn't stack** — Each foot costs 1 extra foot, but several sources of difficult terrain in one space don't make it cost more. Crawling, climbing or swimming without the matching speed add their own extra cost, and those extra costs do add up. [GLOSS]
- **Jumping 2024** — A long jump covers up to your Strength score in feet after a 10 ft run-up (half from standing). A high jump is 3 + Strength modifier (half from standing). Every foot jumped costs movement. Landing in difficult terrain needs a DC 10 Acrobatics check or you fall prone. Step of the Wind doubles jump distance. (2024 change) [GLOSS; WIKI monk:main]
- **Teleportation** — It costs no movement and triggers no OA, and your gear comes with you. If the destination is occupied you appear in the nearest free space. Misty Step and Moonlight Step are not movement. [GLOSS; WIKI druid:moon]
- **Forced movement** — Being pushed or pulled doesn't trigger OAs and doesn't use your speed. It can still trigger area entries (Spike Growth, Spirit Guardians, Web) on someone else's turn. [SAC (2014 carry-over); KIBBLES #17; KIBBLES #15]
- **Dragging a grappled creature** — The grappler pays extra movement unless the target is much smaller. With no half-speed penalty, a fast Monk can drag an enemy through damaging terrain over and over. (2024 change) [GLOSS; KIBBLES #15]
- **Dash twice** — Dash as a Bonus Action and Dash as an action add together. Dash adds your current speed after modifiers, and a speed of 0 means Dash adds nothing. [SAC #19]
- **Flying and prone** — A flyer that is knocked prone, reduced to speed 0 or Incapacitated falls, unless it can Hover. A flyer without Hover can stay in place in the air without moving. [GLOSS; SAC #16]
- **Falling** — Falling deals 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 ft (up to 20d6) and you land prone unless you take no damage. A water landing with a successful check halves it. Slow Fall reduces it by 5 × Monk level as a Reaction. [GLOSS; WIKI monk:main]
- **Controlled mount** — A controlled mount's initiative changes to match yours when you mount it, and it can only Dash, Disengage or Dodge. An intelligent mount acts on its own. An OA provoked by the mount can target either you or the mount. [https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/dungeons-dragons-discussion/rules-game-mechanics/215511-how-does-mounted-combat-work?comment=2; https://blackcitadelrpg.com/mounted-combat-in-dnd-5e/]
- **Mounted feats and redirects** — Mounted Combatant's Veer lets you redirect an attack aimed at your mount to yourself. Charger's charge attack works while mounted only on a controlled mount. [SAC #80; https://www.enworld.org/threads/horse-warrior-shenanigans.708301/]
- **Moving through other creatures' spaces** — You can pass through a prone enemy's space only if it is Incapacitated, Tiny, or two or more sizes different from you. Squeezing into a smaller space no longer exists in 2024, so old species text points to a missing rule. (2024 change) [SAC #17; https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/bugs-support/234299-question-about-2024-rules-compatibility-for-tashas]
- **Compelled Duel leash** — The target can't move more than 30 ft from you by any means, which includes forced movement and teleportation. [SAC #116]
- **Damage doesn't stop movement** — A hit doesn't stop a moving creature unless a feature such as Sentinel's speed 0 or a drop to 0 HP does. [SAC #23]
- **Moving the summoned pack** — When you move, you can also move Conjure Animals' pack up to 30 ft, which can trigger saves on the way. [WIKI spell:conjure-animals]
- **Movement before and after attacks** — Instinctive Pounce gives up to half speed as part of entering Rage. Tactical Shift gives half speed without OAs when you use Second Wind. Cunning Strike's Withdraw gives half speed after the attack. Movement is attached to other actions. [WIKI barbarian:main; WIKI fighter:main; WIKI rogue:main]
- **Travel pace isn't used in combat** — Exploration movement and combat movement are separate systems. [SAC #18]

## 12. Vision and light

- **Heavily obscured means effectively Blinded** — You count as Blinded when trying to see into a heavily obscured space. Fog or Darkness on both sides gives advantage and disadvantage that cancel. [GLOSS; https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/52099/roleplaying-games/dd-2024-hiding-invisibility]
- **Magical darkness vs darkvision** — Darkvision is blocked only when the effect's text says so (the Darkness spell does). Other magical darkness doesn't block it. Each darkness effect needs a flag for this. [SAC #6; WIKI spell:darkness http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:darkness]
- **Darkness dispels weaker light** — Darkness overlapping light from a spell of 2nd level or lower dispels that light. Daylight dispels darkness from spells of its level or lower. Opposing area spells interact by spell level. [WIKI spell:darkness]
- **Darkness on an object** — Cast on an object nobody holds, it becomes an emanation that moves with the object and is blocked if the object is covered. The area of effect follows an object. [WIKI spell:darkness; https://rpgbot.net/2024-dnd/classes/warlock/invocations/]
- **Devil's Sight** — You see normally in magical and normal darkness out to 120 ft. Whether the 2024 version also covers dim light is (disputed). It doesn't help against Fog Cloud or other non-darkness blindness. (2024 change) [https://rpgbot.net/2024-dnd/classes/warlock/invocations/; https://blackcitadelrpg.com/devils-sight-5e/]
- **Blindsight** — Within its range it perceives without sight, sees invisible creatures and through darkness, but not through total cover. It also lets you make an OA against an unseen creature. [GLOSS; SAC #29]
- **Truesight** — Within range it sees through both kinds of darkness, sees invisible creatures, sees through visual illusions, reveals transformations and sees into the Ethereal Plane. [GLOSS]
- **Invisibility doesn't hide your location** — Attackers may still know where you are, so the engine must separate "can see" from "knows location". [https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/52099/roleplaying-games/dd-2024-hiding-invisibility]
- **See Invisibility also reveals hidden creatures** — Because hiding grants the Invisible condition, See Invisibility exposes hidden creatures too. That is probably unintended. (disputed) [KIBBLES #14]
- **Unseen sources** — Frightened doesn't apply if you can't see the source. You can't OA what you can't see. Effects that need a target "you can see" fail against an invisible one. [SAC #12; SAC #29]
- **Portent has no range limit** — It works on any creature you can see, at any distance, even on another plane. "Can see" doesn't mean "within range". [SAC #75]
- **Illusion Wizard fake cover** — Minor Illusion can create an object to hide behind, so you can hide every turn. Minor Illusion's objects don't move and can't be fog or other atmosphere. [KIBBLES #27; SAC #131; SAC #132]
- **Mirror Image uses your AC** — The duplicates use the caster's AC, so a high-AC caster gets far more from them. [KIBBLES #37]
- **Divine Sense** — It doesn't detect Aasimar or Tieflings, because they are Humanoids. Detection goes by creature type, not by flavour. [SAC #57]

## 13. Items: attunement, charges, cursed items, artifacts

- **Attunement limits and duplicates** — You can attune to at most three items, and never to two copies of the same item. Bonuses from different attuned items stack. [SAC #159]
- **Attunement class requirements** — "Requires a spellcaster" is met only if you can cast at least one spell through your own traits or features, not through an item. [ITEMS]
- **Cursed attunement** — You can't end attunement to a cursed item voluntarily until the curse is broken (for example with Remove Curse). Identify and most other methods don't reveal the curse, so the curse is a hidden state revealed later. [ITEMS; https://rpgbot.net/dnd5/how-to-play/magic-items/]
- **Identify shows charges but not curses** — An attuned creature knows the charges and the recharge rate. Charge information is visible to some characters and not to others. [ITEMS]
- **Dawn recharge and destruction** — Charges return at "the next dawn", or when the DM says on planes with no dawn. Wands and staffs roll a d20 when their last charge is used and can be destroyed. [ITEMS]
- **Command words and Silence** — Command words must be audible, so an item can't be activated inside Silence. Most activations are a Magic action. [ITEMS]
- **Spells from items** — They use the lowest level and need no slot, components unless stated, or concentration unless the spell needs it. If you have several casting abilities, you choose which the item uses. [ITEMS; SAC #155]
- **Potions are a Bonus Action** — Drinking a potion, or giving one to an adjacent creature, takes a Bonus Action, even if the creature is unconscious. Potions need concentration only if they say so or copy a concentration spell. (2024 change) [SAC #156; SAC #157; https://arcaneeye.com/dnd-items/healing-potions-5e-2024/]
- **Mixing potions** — Mixing potions calls for a d100 miscibility roll. The result can be an explosion, both potions cancelling out, or one effect becoming permanent. It is revealed only once it becomes evident. [ITEMS]
- **Sentient item conflict** — When the bearer acts against the item's goals, the bearer makes a Charisma save against 12 + the item's Charisma modifier. On a failure the item makes demands, then may block attunement, suppress its properties or Charm the bearer for 1d12 hours. The DM controls the item. [ITEMS]
- **Artifacts** — They are immune to damage except by their unique method of destruction. Their random properties need per-instance rolled data. [ITEMS]
- **Magic items resist damage** — Most magic items (not potions or scrolls) have resistance to all damage. [ITEMS]
- **Shields must be donned** — Merely holding a magic shield gives none of its bonus. Donning or doffing a shield takes an action (errata, closing a trick of swapping shields every turn). Attacking with a shield keeps its AC bonus. (2024 change) [SAC #163; SAC #89; ERRATA; KIBBLES #6]
- **Magic ranged weapon, plain ammunition** — The attack counts as magical even with nonmagical ammunition. A thrown magic weapon comes back only if its text says so. [SAC #160; SAC #161]
- **Ammunition and Loading** — After a fight you can recover half your spent ammunition. Because drawing a weapon is free with an attack, you can draw a fresh crossbow for each shot, which makes Loading nearly pointless. [KIBBLES #9; https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/]
- **Paired and worn items** — Both items of a pair must be worn, unless the DM allows otherwise. The DM decides whether unusual anatomy, including Wild Shape forms, can use an item. [ITEMS; WIKI druid:main]
- **Sphere of Annihilation and Tiny Hut** — The sphere can enter a Tiny Hut and destroys its dome. Item and spell interactions sometimes need hard-coded exceptions. [SAC #162]
- **Evolving magic items (optional rule)** — Items that gain properties as the owner progresses need per-instance state over time. [WIKI evolving-magic-items http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/evolving-magic-items]

## 14. Homebrew

- **Optional rules dropped from the 2024 DMG** — Flanking, hero points, plot points and hitting cover were removed, but many tables still use them. The engine needs toggles for rules that are no longer in any book. [https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/axed-optional-rules]
- **Flanking toggle** — Flanking gives advantage to allies on opposite sides of an enemy. It needs position and adjacency data that a character sheet doesn't have, so it must accept manual input. [https://www.themonstersknow.com/965-2/]
- **Critical fumbles** — Rolling a 1 has a house-ruled consequence. Officially, a 1 on an ability check or save isn't an automatic failure, and a 1 on an attack is just a miss. [SAC #3]
- **Potions: house rule reversed** — The popular "potions as a Bonus Action" house rule became the official rule in 2024, so some tables now house-rule the opposite (drink yourself as a Bonus Action, feed an ally as an action). [https://www.enworld.org/threads/weird-potion-house-rule-idea.714249/; https://rpgbot.net/openvideo/v/potions-are-a-bonus-action-in-dnd-now]
- **Gritty rests and long-term timers** — Variant rest lengths (a week for a Long Rest, overnight for a Short Rest) change every "per rest" counter. Rest length must be a setting, not a constant. [GLOSS (rest definitions); https://www.enworld.org/threads/2024-house-rules-fixes.706402/]
- **Tracking exhaustion your own way** — Tables use the 10-level playtest exhaustion or another homebrew scale, so Foundry modules let you replace the built-in track. [https://github.com/Larkinabout/fvtt-dnd5e-custom-counters/releases/tag/1.2.0]
- **Mixed 2014 and 2024 content** — Older material that the 2024 books didn't replace (Silvery Barbs, Absorb Elements, Tasha's content) can still be used. Same-named 2014 and 2024 versions must not both apply. [https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/bugs-support/234299-question-about-2024-rules-compatibility-for-tashas; https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/]
- **Custom spells that scale** — Homebrew spells copy 2024 patterns (stat blocks scaling with level, extra targets when upcast for Twinned, emanations). Custom content needs the same templates as official content. [WIKI spell:summon-beast; https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1769-2024-sorcerer-vs-2014-sorcerer-whats-new]
- **Custom monsters and partial shape-change** — Homebrew shape-changers need to choose which stats change, which Foundry can't do. [FVTT#7153]
- **Familiars that can attack** — Some DMs allow it, so Foundry asks for an option instead of a fixed rule. [FVTT#4009]
- **Fixing the hide rules** — Many tables rewrite Hide and Invisible. The visibility model should be a configurable policy. [https://www.enworld.org/threads/reworking-the-2024-hiding-rules.712813/; https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/dungeons-dragons-discussion/homebrew-house-rules/205351-common-sense-rules-for-stealth-hiding-and]
- **Other optional rules in the 2024 books** — Bastions, Circle Magic, Factions and Renown, and evolving items are toggleable systems with their own resources. [WIKI home http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/]
- **Sheet overrides** — Players manually override AC when the sheet gets it wrong, for example Mage Armor or Unarmored Defense under armour on D&D Beyond. A homebrew or manual override must sit above computed values and stay visible. [https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/bugs-support/5619-mage-armor-not-updating-ac?comment=42]
- **Opt-in bonuses** — Some bonuses apply only when the player chooses (situational feats, "you can" clauses). Foundry users ask for opt-in toggles. [FVTT#7255]
- **Death-save variants** — Tables change the number of successes or failures needed. [FVTT#7367]

## 15. Multi-target and area damage

- **One damage roll, many saves** — An area spell rolls damage once and applies it to every target. Each target saves separately and gets full or half damage. [https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/]
- **Resistance per target** — The same rolled number is then halved, doubled or ignored per target (Resistance, Vulnerability, Immunity, Elemental Adept). Each target runs its own pipeline: save result, modifiers, Resistance, Vulnerability, Ward, temp HP, HP. [SAC #36; WIKI feat:elemental-adept]
- **Evasion** — A successful Dexterity save for half damage means no damage, and a failure means half. It doesn't work while Incapacitated, but does work while surprised. [WIKI rogue:main; WIKI monk:main; SAC #60]
- **Careful Spell and Sculpt-style protection** — Chosen allies automatically succeed and, in 2024, take no damage. This adds an "auto-succeed and no damage" outcome. (2024 change) [https://dungeonmister.com/guides/classes-in-dungeons-dragons/sorcerer-metamagic-dnd-5e-2024/]
- **Allies inside the area** — Fireball and Shatter hit everyone, allies included. Choosing where to place the origin is the only RAW protection (Shatter). [SAC #91]
- **Area shapes and the origin** — Spheres and cylinders include their point of origin. Cones, cubes, emanations and lines don't, unless their creator decides otherwise. An obstruction moves an unseen origin to its near side. [GLOSS]
- **Cover in areas** — Half and three-quarters cover add to Dexterity saves as well as AC, and total cover from the origin protects you completely. Only the best degree of cover counts. A creature in between gives half cover, not disadvantage. [GLOSS; SAC #34]
- **Multi-ray spells resolve one at a time** — Scorching Ray and Eldritch Blast beams can be declared together, but each attack, crit, Hex rider and Agonizing Blast bonus resolves separately. [SAC #97]
- **Per-spell bonuses vs per-ray bonuses** — Elemental Affinity applies once per spell. Conjure Minor Elementals applies to every ray. Empowered's die limit covers the whole spell. [SAC #63; SAC #64; https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/conjure-minor-elementals-nerf]
- **Magic Missile splitting** — The darts hit at once and can be split between targets. Shield blocks all of them, and Cutting Words can reduce their damage. Whether the darts share one damage roll and count as one instance for concentration is (disputed). [WIKI spell:shield; SAC #49]
- **Uncanny Dodge doesn't work on areas** — It halves an attack's damage only, never a saving-throw area. Evasion is the area defence. [SAC #59]
- **Guardian of Faith counts damage dealt** — Its damage cap counts damage actually taken after saves (a halved 10 counts as 10). Each target's final damage feeds back into the source's counter. [SAC #125]
- **Spirit Guardians picks who it ignores** — It spares creatures you choose, and the once-per-turn save is tracked per creature. Moving it onto enemies counts as entering their space. [https://www.enworld.org/threads/spirt-guardians.706656/]
- **Concentration saves from area damage** — Every concentrating creature hit by one Fireball makes its own save with its own DC (half its own final damage). [GLOSS]
- **Cleave and Nick** — Cleave (once per turn) hits a second creature next to the first, without your ability modifier to damage. Nick folds the Light-weapon extra attack into the Attack action, once per turn. These are multi-target weapon riders. [KIBBLES #1; KIBBLES #2; SAC #39; SAC #81]
- **Topple and Polearm Master saves** — Topple forces a save on every hit, which adds up to many saves per turn with bonus-action attacks. [KIBBLES #11]
- **Objects in the area** — Objects use AC and HP tables, are immune to poison and psychic damage, may have a damage threshold, and fail every save. [GLOSS]

## 16. DM adjudication

- **Influence** — A check is needed only when the creature is hesitant. The DC is 15 or the monster's Intelligence score, whichever is higher, and after a failure you must wait 24 hours. The DM decides the creature's attitude first. (2024 change) [GLOSS]
- **Help** — It gives an ally advantage on one ability check, or on an attack against an enemy within 5 ft of you. The benefit expires at the start of your next turn, and it still applies after you move away. (2024 change) [GLOSS; SAC #21]
- **Surprise 2024** — A surprised creature simply has disadvantage on initiative and doesn't lose a turn. An invisible creature has advantage on initiative. Surprise is decided by the DM. (2024 change) [GLOSS; https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/]
- **Improvised actions** — Anyone can try to disarm, but how is the DM's call. Only Battle Masters have a written rule. Disarming Attack can't remove a donned shield. [SAC #38; SAC #54]
- **Natural 1 on checks** — Officially not an automatic failure, but many DMs rule it is. [SAC #3]
- **Dispelling and what counts as magic** — An effect is magical if its text says so (spells and items always are). Dispel Magic ends spells, not other magical effects such as Wild Shape. It ends one target's share of a spell and can't affect instantaneous spells. Magic Resistance does apply against Channel Divinity. [SAC #9; SAC #118; SAC #119; SAC #122; SAC #150]
- **Divine Intervention vs long casting times** — Whether it can cast spells like Hallow through a Magic action is unclear. (disputed) [KIBBLES #13]
- **Somatic components and free hands** — You need a free hand for material components, and the same hand can do the somatic gestures. Players argue over how War Caster's somatic clause fits this. (disputed) [SAC #107; KIBBLES #26]
- **The DM resolves Wild Shape gear, anatomy and planes** — Whether gear fits a beast, whether odd anatomy can use an item, and when "dawn" comes on another plane are all the DM's call. The engine must accept manual rulings. [WIKI druid:main; ITEMS]
- **Simultaneous effects order** — The creature whose turn it is chooses the order. [GLOSS]
- **Suggestion's wording** — 2024 removed the "reasonable" requirement, so the DM decides what an acceptable suggestion is. [KIBBLES #33; SAC #142]
- **Suffocation** — You can hold your breath for 1 + Constitution modifier minutes (minimum 30 seconds), then gain a level of Exhaustion each turn. [GLOSS]
- **Environmental hazards** — Falling, suffocation and DMG hazards deal damage from no creature, so they must enter the damage pipeline without an attacker. [GLOSS]
- **Planes and extradimensional spaces** — A creature inside an extradimensional space isn't on any plane, which matters for "same plane" checks such as Find Familiar's link and concentration-style limits. [SAC #8]
- **Feats past level 20** — Level 20 is the cap. DMs can give extra feats or Epic Boons as further advancement. [SAC #11]
- **Exhaustion in chases** — The errata clarified how exhaustion is gained and recovered in chases. [ERRATA]
- **Wall of Force and Forcecage** — Only specific powerful magic gets through them, so the DM must adjudicate attempts. [KIBBLES #24]

## 17. At-the-table gaps

- **Physical dice entered by hand** — Every roll (attack, damage, save, death save, initiative, recharge, wild magic, item destruction d20) needs a "typed result" path. Advantage needs two values. Rerolls (Heroic Inspiration, Lucky, Indomitable) must replace a hand-entered value. Minimums (Reliable Talent, GWF 3s, Elemental Adept 2s) need the raw die faces, not just a total. [SAC #61; WIKI rogue:main]
- **Hand-entered crits** — If the player says "nat 20", the app must double every die attached to the attack, including riders added afterwards such as Divine Smite. With physical dice the player may roll the extra dice and report only a total. [GLOSS; WIKI spell:divine-smite]
- **Damage typed in without a type breakdown** — A typed "23 damage" loses the per-type split needed for Resistance (for example fire + radiant from a Primal Strike or a smite). The app must accept either a single total or a total split by type. [WIKI druid:main (Primal Strike); SAC #36]
- **Monster kills and who dealt them** — Kill credit matters for rules: Hex and Hunter's Mark move when their target drops to 0, Great Weapon Master's Hew triggers on a kill, and "reduce to 0" features fire. The killer must be recorded even for monsters tracked only by the DM. [WIKI spell:hex]
- **Wild Shape mid-combat** — The player transforms as a Bonus Action and gains temp HP (choosing whether to replace existing ones). Concentration continues and the stat block swaps while the player's own HP stays live. The form ends on Incapacitated, and leftover temp HP must be handled on revert. Concentration saves now use the beast's Constitution. [WIKI druid:main; WIKI druid:moon; FVTT#5962]
- **Area spells that hit allies** — A Fireball that hits two PCs and three monsters: the caster enters one damage roll, and each PC applies their own save, Evasion, Resistance and concentration save. The app must accept one roll that several sheets apply, and record friendly fire in the session log. [SAC #91; WIKI rogue:main]
- **Creatures outside the combat tracker** — Familiars (own initiative), summons (immediately after the caster), controlled mounts (rider's initiative), the Beast Master beast (acts on your turn), Unseen Servant (no initiative) and DM-only NPCs all affect PCs without being "in" the app. Effects such as Life Bond healing or Find Steed's Healing Touch, and damage from untracked creatures, must still be recordable. [WIKI spell:find-familiar; WIKI spell:summon-beast; WIKI ranger:beast-master; SAC #145]
- **Retroactive reactions at the table** — The table resolves the hit, then the player remembers Shield or Uncanny Dodge. The app must allow an undo or amend of an already applied hit and its damage. [WIKI spell:shield]
- **Initiative triggers when rolled physically** — When a player types an initiative roll, the app must still fire Persistent Rage, Uncanny Metabolism, Evergreen Wild Shape and Alert swaps, and apply Exhaustion and Incapacitated to the roll. [WIKI barbarian:main; WIKI monk:main; WIKI feat:alert]
- **Per-turn limits on others' turns** — The app must know whose turn it is (even with no tracker running) to allow a second Sneak Attack on an OA, or a second Spirit Guardians save on an ally's turn. [SAC #58; https://www.enworld.org/threads/spirt-guardians.706656/]
- **Hidden curses and DM-only state** — A cursed item looks normal to the player until revealed. The app needs DM-only fields on items the player owns. [ITEMS]
- **DM-adjudicated outcomes** — Influence attitudes, improvised actions, gear that fits in Wild Shape and disputed Hide results all need a "DM ruled: X" entry instead of a computed result. [GLOSS; SAC #38]
- **Owlbear and VTT damage applied outside the app** — When the DM applies damage on the VTT, the sheet must reconcile HP and temp HP and still prompt a concentration save (the DC comes from damage before temp HP). [SAC #109; FVTT#5394]
- **Lingering penalties across sessions** — Raise Dead's −4 decays per Long Rest, and Exhaustion and temp HP last across rests. Rests taken off-app need a "we long-rested" button that processes every timer. [WIKI spell:raise-dead; GLOSS]
