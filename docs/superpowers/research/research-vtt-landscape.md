# Where the industry draws the line between the "brain" (sheet + rules) and the "map" (VTT) — D&D 5e/2024, state as of 2026-09-03

Research brief for the d20 Folio combat re-architecture. Question: which products automate which combat mechanics, how sheet and map talk to each other, which pairings win, and what a free, offline-first, phone-first companion with a deterministic rules engine should take from it.

Method: WebSearch + WebFetch of primary docs, vendor blogs/changelogs, forum threads (D&D Beyond forums, EN World, Roll20 forums, Fantasy Grounds boards, Steam, AMO/App Store reviews), plus headless screenshots into `refs4/` (see §6). Reddit could not be fetched directly; Reddit sentiment is triangulated through EN World, D&D Beyond forums, blogs that quote it, and store reviews. YouTube transcripts were not fetchable; behaviour of reaction dialogs is taken from module READMEs and settings docs instead. Every dated claim carries its source in §7.

---

## 0. Executive summary

1. **No product in the market automates opportunity attacks from geometry except Foundry + midi-qol + a premades pack (Gambit's/CPR).** Everyone else — including Fantasy Grounds, Shard, Roll20, DDB Maps, Owlbear, Avrae, Shieldmaiden — treats "who is in reach" as a human fact and, at most, gives you a button to _record_ the reaction after the human decided it happened.
2. **The line between brain and map has moved toward the brain.** The 2024–2026 wave (Roll20 2024 sheet, Foundry dnd5e v4 "Activities", DDB Character Sheets in Maps, Shard "Powers", AC5e) is about the _sheet_ owning conditions, effects, concentration, resources and even range/reach modifiers — the map is reduced to "distance oracle + token HP/condition display".
3. **The winning pairings are asymmetric:** (a) all-in-one Foundry for automation maximalists (one-time $50, but "we spend more time with the VTT than playing" is the canonical complaint); (b) DDB sheet + a thin map (DDB Maps, AboveVTT, Owlbear+GMG) for the mainstream, where the sheet is the brain and the map is deliberately dumb; (c) companion-only trackers (Shieldmaiden, Improved Initiative, Avrae) for theatre-of-the-mind, which automate everything _except_ position.
4. **For d20 Folio the evidence points to architecture (c) upgraded with a minimal spatial model — an adjacency/engagement graph ("who is engaged with whom", plus optional 3–4 range bands) — not a grid.** That is exactly the information Foundry's OA automation actually consumes (a token leaving a reach set), it is what theatre-of-the-mind DMs already track by hand, and it is the one thing no companion app offers today.

## 1. Product-by-product findings

Legend for automation claims: **A** = automated from data with no human judgement; **P** = prompted/assisted (the tool asks, human decides); **R** = recorded only (human decides and does; tool tracks the consequence); **—** = absent.

### 1.1 Foundry VTT (core + dnd5e system v4/v5) and the automation stack

**What the core system does (no modules).** dnd5e 4.0 (Sept 2024, shipped with the 2024 PHB module) introduced _Activities_ — "a single Item [can] house any number of different activations", each with attack/save/damage/enchant/summon sub-steps, targeting and template definitions, and per-activity concentration. dnd5e 5.0 (2025) added SRD 5.2 content; 5.3.x (2025–26) is current. Core system gives: concentration tracking (effects tied to the concentration item are removed together), condition/status effects with "Ruletips" for cover and terrain, requested rolls, party actor, vision modes. Foundry v13 core (2025) added _Token Drag Measurement_ with waypoints and _Modify Movement Cost_ regions "to implement system-specific rules such as difficult terrain". Core does **not** auto-target, auto-apply damage, check range, or fire reactions.

**midi-qol** (the automation kernel). From the README (v13.x, 2025–26): "Check Range" — _None / Long range fail / Long range disadvantage_, with three distance metrics (center-to-center, grid-square, token perimeter) and elevation; "Auto Target on Template" — _None / Always / Walls Block / Walled Templates_; auto check hits, auto roll damage, auto save checking, "Auto apply damage with undo card" (GM gets a per-target damage card with resistances resolved); concentration: "Taking damage causes a concentration check, failure removes concentration" and "any effects due to the spell on any tokens (self + targets) are removed"; **Reactions**: "Do Reactions" / "GM Do Reactions", "Reaction Timeout — how many seconds before auto-rolling if no reaction selected", "Show Reaction Chat Message", trigger points `preAttack, isAttacked, isMissed, isHit, isDamaged, isHealed, isSave…`; "Enforce Reactions" ("query the user if they want to continue because they have already used their reaction"); optional rule "Record Opportunity Attacks — applies a reaction marker when an actor makes an attack roll when it is not their turn"; notably _"When recording Attacks of Opportunity, range checking for that attack is disabled"_ — midi records OA, it does not _detect_ it.

**Gambit's Premades / Chris's Premades (CPR)** are what actually detect OA. Gambit's README: "This automates opportunity attacks while taking into account … Sentinel, War Caster, Polearm Master, and Battle Master Brace. This feature will only function while actors are in combat." Package page: "Custom AppV2 Dialogs for reaction automations with an integrated and animated countdown timer … Optional customization to display reaction dialogs … to both the GM and Player. AFK player? No problem." (v2.1.44, Aug 2026, Foundry 11–13). CPR lists Counterspell/OA/Silvery Barbs as "very difficult to handle '3rd party reaction' items" and requires dnd5e + midi + DAE + socketlib + Times Up. In other words: **OA detection is a 4–5-module stack on top of a paid VTT, maintained by two volunteers.**

**AC5e (Automated Conditions 5e)** — the 2025 alternative to midi for people who want less: conditions (Blinded, Frightened, Prone, Restrained, Exhaustion 1–5, …) auto-apply advantage/disadvantage to the correct roll button; "Range profile changes such as short/long/reach and out-of-range fail", "nearby-foe disadvantage" on ranged attacks, auras. Does not need midi. It computes range from token distance but **does not trigger reactions**.

**Range/reach visualisation modules.** _Combat Range Overlay_ (Nazrax): colour-codes squares reachable in 1/2 actions and circles enemies by "how many moves are needed to attack them: white = immediate…"; computes pathfinding distance, squares only. _Elevation Ruler_ (caewok): drag ruler, pathfinding, elevation — largely absorbed by v13 core drag-measure. _Terrain Ruler / Enhanced Terrain Layer_: difficult-terrain cost. _Alternative Token Cover_ (caewok): "GM can define low, medium, and high levels of cover and associate each level with an active effect"; with midi it applies cover to the attack roll and to templates. _Token Magic FX_ is purely visual filters (misfiled in the brief as a range tool). _Automated Animations + JB2A_ = visuals; _DAE_ = the effect engine that "reads the spell's data and automatically applies an effect to the targets". _Ready Set Roll_ = quick roll cards, explicitly **incompatible** with midi ("I will not install Midi QOL in games where Ready Set Roll is used"). _DDB Importer_ (MrPrimate): imports DDB characters, spells, items free; monsters/classes/feats on Patreon; can "sync limited character changes back to DDB" for patrons.

**Sheet↔map communication:** one process; the actor _is_ the token's data; templates are scene objects that resolve to target sets; every roll is a workflow with hooks. This is why Foundry can do OA: the reach set of every token is computable at movement time.

**What users praise/hate.** Praise: "spells, attacks, and conditions all handle themselves with minimal DM intervention" (EN World, Aug 2025, 8.5/10); "one-time purchase". Hate: "How many folks actually want to have to run an AWS server" (EN World, 2020); "We spend more time with the vtt than actually playing" (EN World "VTT of choice", 2025); "won't flawlessly automate combat … without extensive maintenance"; midi "tends to be a challenge for new players"; every dnd5e major (3.0, 4.0, v13, v14) breaks the module stack for weeks ("v14 compatibility is still new so caution is advised", midi page 2026). Reaction timeouts exist precisely because prompt-storms stall the table (default suggestions: 20–30 s).

**Pricing:** $50 one-time license; hosting optional (The Forge $4.49–$12.99/mo); players free.

### 1.2 Roll20 (2024 sheet, Jumpgate, Beyond20)

**What the 2024 sheet automates** (Roll20 blog, Nov 22 2024): drag-drop items/feats "effects and bonuses are applied instantly"; attunement/equipped gating; "toggles effects and conditions that automatically adjust your rolls, whether it's adding bonuses, applying disadvantage, or rolling with advantage"; weapon masteries in roll templates; HP +/- button; rest resets; upcast prompts. _Condition Sync_ (Pro only, 2025): a condition toggled on the sheet marks the token and vice-versa — then was temporarily disabled for a stacking bug. "D&D 2024 Automations are Here" (Nov 13 2025) is about **token actions and macros**, e.g. a GM macro that rolls saves for _selected_ tokens (see `refs4/crops/roll20-automations-2.png`: a "Macro Options — choose saving throw" dialog next to a token). Concentration checks on damage are **not** native to the 2024 (Beacon) sheet — the community Concentration API script "does not play nice" with Beacon sheets. Jumpgate (default since Jan 2025) is an engine/perf rewrite: "Automatically Measure Token Movement" toggle in the measure tool, dynamic lighting (Plus+), faster canvas; third-party SmartAoE script "don't play nicely" with it. **Auto-targeting of templates and automatic damage application remain long-standing feature requests, not features.**

**What stays manual:** every distance check, every reach/OA, AoE membership, cover, damage application to NPC tokens (API script or hand), concentration.

**Beyond20** (browser extension): "Beyond20 only has access to the information displayed on the page"; rolls flow DDB → VTT chat; HP sync requires name matching and "Update VTT Token HP"; known bugs "syncs to the character sheet in Roll20 but not to the token", multi-token cases; "no way … to adjust HP on monster/NPC tokens on the fly". v2.18.0 (2025) finally added HP sync to the Roll20 2024 sheet template. It surfaces nothing about reach: it is a one-way roll pipe with a toast/chat card on the VTT side.

**Praise/hate:** "Super easy to set up … you just send a link" vs "essential features locked behind Pro", lag on big maps, memory leak on advanced sheets (Roll20 performance posts Aug 2026). **Pricing:** free tier; Plus $5.99/mo ($59.99/yr); Pro $10.99/mo ($109.99/yr); new "Elite" $150/yr. Dynamic lighting = Plus; API and Condition Sync = Pro.

### 1.3 D&D Beyond Maps (and the Sigil postscript)

Timeline (DDB posts/changelog): Encounter tracking in Maps Oct 29 2024 ("populated automatically based on the tokens present on the map"; initiative auto from creature score or "Auto Initiative"; players see order and whose turn); monster stat blocks + monster HP tracking Dec 10 2024 (clickable HP → damage/heal/temp/max override — see `refs4/crops/ddb-adjusting-hp-frame.png`); Maps free for all tiers Sept 16 2025; 3D/shared dice Feb 2026; **Character Sheets in Maps Apr 2026** ("changes made on your full Character Sheet — like adjusting HP, equipping items, or casting spells — will automatically show up in Maps"; "if a player changes their character's Hit Points from their character sheet, their Hit Points in the Combat Encounter menu will automatically update"); initiative auto-updates when players roll Jun 29 2026; **Condition Tracking Jul 23 2026** ("syncing to and from Character Sheets" — with a note that you may need to refresh; the post does _not_ say conditions change any roll — user questions asking that went unanswered); Bloodied/Dead visuals at 50%/0% HP Aug 17 2026; Scene Prep Aug 31 2026. Roadmap 2026: Rules Assistant, Campaign Console, prep tools, Quickbuilder. Sigil (3D) lost ~90% of its team Mar 2025 and was formally shut down (servers off Oct 31 2026 per WotC).

**What Maps does not do:** no measurement-driven rules, no AoE targeting, no auto-damage from rolls, no reach/OA, no cover, no rules effects from conditions, no HP bars on tokens ("Forum members clarified that Maps VTT lacks automatic HP visualization", Oct 2025), custom map upload is Master-tier only, encounter difficulty uses 2024 DMG math (and was reported "using the maximum as a minimum"). DDB's own framing: "much more minimalist, focusing on a low barrier to entry" (DDB staff Davyd, May 10 2025); users: "DDB maps has hardly any VTT features, I would say it is more for theatre of the mind support" (May 12 2025); "Two out of six features delivered while we've completed 10 of the 12 months" (Nov 13 2025); "The confidence to performance ratio is misaligned" (Sept 11 2025). The Vice write-up (May 26 2026) still concludes it "doesn't go quite as deep as VTTs like Roll20 or Foundry".

**Pricing:** Maps free; Hero $2.99/mo, Master $5.99/mo (Master = custom maps/homebrew tokens, content sharing).

**Reception summary:** the market welcomed _sheet-as-brain + map-as-display_ — the praise is always "point and click, super intuitive"; the complaints are always the missing rules glue (HP visualisation, AoE, dice → damage). Nobody asks DDB for OA automation; they ask for HP rings.

### 1.4 Alchemy RPG

Positioned as "the world's first VTT built specifically for … theater of the mind"; "switching to Tactical Mode is a click away" (grid, snap, fog, measuring tool, tokens). Character sheet elevated into "prominent UI panels". Numtini review (Jan 14 2025): "Automation is severely lacking … Combat requires excessive clicking across multiple tabs", tactical mode "lacks essential features like fog of war and NPC placement" (older build), "unfinished product charging premium prices"; Chandler (Medium) chose it _over_ Foundry to "play your game not the VTT"; Churape (Apr 2025): "perfect fit for our theater-of-the-mind games", but "other VTT's handle combat and battle maps better". No 2024-rules statement found. **Pricing:** free (3 games/3 PCs/1 universe); Unlimited $8/mo or $88/yr. Sheet↔map link: the sheet is the primary UI, the map is a scene mode; no rules bridging (no auto-target, no reach).

### 1.5 Shard Tabletop

The most fully modelled 5e sheet outside Foundry: "Feature Modeling" declares mechanics as data (AC bonus, extra attack, dueling, resistances, movement types…); "Shard Powers" (Dec 21 2022) added actions that "track duration and automatically apply bonuses", conditions assigned by features, multi-type damage totals, and the GM can "make saves for all of the creatures in an area of effect and then apply full or half damage based on the players damage roll"; "Save + Damage" release note added cantrip zero-on-save and put save+damage in the token damage menu; 2024 PHB modelling shipped Jan 16 2025 (reaction/bonus-action spell filters, rest highlighting, shape-change HP). Gnome Stew (Oct 7 2024): initiative, spell duration and concentration monitoring, conditions on tokens with visual indicators; damage by hovering the token and picking a recent roll; "no dynamic lighting", "no official D&D support", learning curve. **Reach/OA/movement:** nothing in docs, release notes or reviews; AoE membership is _GM-selected_ (the AoE is a token you place; the GM picks the creatures). Targeting is from the combat tracker, not from geometry. **Pricing:** free forever tier (SRD+, 300 creatures); Adventurer $2.99/mo, Gamemaster $5.99/mo, Gamemaster Pro $9.99/mo. Sheet↔map: same app, but the sheet drives; the map is a picture with tokens whose HP/conditions mirror the tracker.

### 1.6 Fantasy Grounds Unity

Deepest _effects_ automation of the old guard: dragging spells/items/features onto the sheet "in almost every case creates an effect"; ATK/DMG/AC/SAVE modifiers, resistances, conditions parsed from properly formatted text ("On a failed save, the target is frightened for 1 minute" auto-applies), concentration check on damage, encumbrance, AC from worn gear. Targeting is explicit (target arrows) and, with a grid, "targeting a token will calculate and display the range automatically" — but "Ranges have not been coded into the 5E ruleset"; extensions ("5E Enhancer", "Automatic Flanking and Range" on the Forge store) add long-range/nearby-foe disadvantage. Combat tracker computes creature Size and Reach from effects, yet "Fantasy Grounds doesn't have automatic functions that limit movement of tokens" and an "Attack of Opportunity tracker" thread shows OA is a manual counter. Reviews: "deepest automation available" but "outdated interface with confusing right-click menus; brutal learning curve exceeds Foundry's" (EN World, Aug 2025, 6/10). **Pricing:** software free since Nov 8 2025 (was $50 Ultimate); content paid.

### 1.7 TaleSpire

3D miniatures sandbox, "not tied to a specific game or ruleset"; community "Symbiotes" (browser panels) provide sheets and a DDB game-log watcher (Feb 2025). Steam: Very Positive (~90/100, 5k reviews) but recent reviews (Aug–Sept 2026) say it "desperately needs way more QoL features for running campaigns and automation"; ~$75 for a table of four ($24.99 + seats). Zero rules automation by design.

### 1.8 Owlbear Rodeo 2 + extensions

Core: maps, tokens, fog, drawing, rooms; "Owlbear uses plug-and-play extensions rather than bloating the base software". Relevant extensions: _Game Master's Grimoire_ (statblocks from Tabletop Almanac; HP/AC/temp HP on tokens; initiative roll from statblock modifiers; limited-use tracking; dddice; **D&D Beyond importer** — "re-import the character sheet after a level up … a system … compares the latest sync state and … resolves/merges" — i.e. sync by re-import, not live); _Stat Bubbles_; _Initiative Tracker_; _Condition Markers_; _AOE Shapes_; _Smoke & Spectre_ (dynamic fog/lighting); _Bendy Ruler / Character Distances / Path Measure / Movement Tracker_; _Sheet from Beyond_ (opens a DDB sheet URL in a popover — "no HP sync or rolls"). See `refs4/crops/obr-gmg-main.png`: GMG's tracker panel with HP/AC/initiative per token and HP labels on the map. Sentiment: "Dead simple, no automation, but it allows me to run everything without issue"; SQYD (Mar 2025): VTT makers are "taking great table top experiences and turning them into bad videogames". **Pricing:** free (2 rooms, 100 MB); Fledgling $3.99/mo ($39.99/yr); Bestling higher (price not captured). Phones/tablets first-class.

### 1.9 AboveVTT

Browser extension that turns the DDB campaign page into a VTT: "use your DDB character sheet from the web or DDB app, with HP, dice rolls, conditions", one-click monster tokens with HP/AC and clickable stat blocks, fog, dynamic lighting, HP "auras" (ring that thins and goes green→red). Firefox reviews 4.6/5 (25): "the easiest way to go from managing character sheets to fighting monsters"; complaints: lag, tokens "move back", disconnects (Feb 2025), maintenance doubts (Mar 2025), and the notable ask for "tighter integration with the Initiative/Combat Tracker and Encounter Builder" as "the last area of duplication". Free. It is the purest example of _sheet-app + thin map_ and it does not attempt reach/OA either.

### 1.10 Tabletop Simulator, Menyr, Arkenforge

_TTS_ ($19.99, per seat): physics sandbox; "there are no rules that the game enforces, and you have to count all points by yourself"; workshop tables add trays/trackers. _Menyr_ (NOG Studio, open beta): procedural 3D worlds, "customizable game system … character stats, spell range, dice macros", combat mode "spawn enemies, move characters around, track initiative, HP", measuring tool; free base promised; not a rules engine. _Arkenforge_ ($35–$250 one-time, "never … a subscription paywall"): in-person map/TV/projector tool with fog, lighting and touch-screen mini detection; explicitly "not a tool for online play or system rules and automation".

### 1.11 Physical-table hybrids (TV/projector + phones)

Observed workflows (DDB forums Mar 2023/Mar 2024, EN World Aug 2025, Lumen Jun 2026): (a) Foundry on a laptop with a second "player" browser on the TV (localhost) — needs a second mouse, awkward for a tablet DM; (b) Owlbear on a laptop, cast to TV, players move tokens from phones — "mobile-friendly", "doesn't have projection capabilities" natively; (c) AboveVTT/DDB Maps on a tablet or laptop with DDB sheets on phones; (d) Arkenforge/Menyr for the visual layer only; (e) DDB app on phones + physical map + a DM tracker (Improved Initiative, Shieldmaiden, Kobold+). **The DDB mobile app still has no encounter/combat tracker** — staff: "We haven't started it yet and there's no ETA" (Feb 2022), repeated Sept 2023; still unanswered in 2024–26 roadmap. _Lumen VTT_ (2026 newcomer) is the first product to make "DM laptop + TV + player phones" the primary target, with "200+ automated 5e spells", "connected combat state", "phone companion controls", and its own acid test: "Put the map on the TV, give one player a phone, cast an area spell, and watch how much the DM still has to manually repair." PrismScroll (4.6/5, 3.2k) is a phone sheet with 2014+2024 support, local-first characters; its reviews never mention combat tracking, maps or party features — it is a sheet, not a brain for the table.

### 1.12 Companion-only apps (no map)

_Shieldmaiden_ (free tier, storage-paid): initiative, HP/temp/max, conditions, reminders that "trigger notifications when certain events occur" (concentration), one-click monster abilities "instantly apply the result to any target" with resistances "automatically halve, double, or ignore", live player view; DDB characters via the "D&D Character Sync" Chrome extension (pull by click, not live). Nothing about position; reactions/OA are not mentioned.
_Improved Initiative_ (free, Patreon extras): initiative, HP, tags with durations ("Stunned until the end of Orc 2's next turn"), Concentrating tag with damage reminder, player view. Reviewed as "barebones and clumsy" (Homebrew Creation, Feb 2024). No position.
_Kobold+ Fight Club_: encounter builder with a light tracker — "not a 'combat tracker' since the DM still has to track all of the combat"; exports to Improved Initiative.
_Avrae_ (Discord): the most automated map-less brain. Automation tree = Attack/Save/Damage/TempHP/IEffect/Condition/Target nodes; `!init attack … -t Goblin1` resolves to-hit vs AC and applies damage; `!i aoo <combatant> <attack> -t <target>` and `!i rc <combatant> <spell>` exist for opportunity attacks and reaction spells — **the human declares the OA; the bot resolves it**. "The documentation contains no movement distance mechanics or positional reach calculations."

## 3. The three dominant architectures — who wins and why

**A. All-in-one VTT (sheet, rules and map in one process).** Foundry (+modules), Fantasy Grounds, Shard, Alchemy, Roll20. _Winner: Foundry_ — because the map is the source of truth for distance, every rule that needs geometry (range, cover, template membership, OA) can be computed, and a data-driven effects model (DAE/Activities) supplies the non-geometric half. _Cost:_ the stack is fragile (system majors break modules), configuration-heavy, desktop-first, and the community's own top complaint is time spent servicing the tool; the second-tier all-in-ones (FGU, Shard) get 80% of the automation with 0% of the geometry.

**B. Sheet-app + VTT bridge (the brain lives in DDB; the map is somewhere else).** DDB sheet + DDB Maps (2026 sheet-in-sidebar, HP/condition sync), DDB + AboveVTT, DDB + Owlbear/GMG (import), DDB + Beyond20 → Roll20/Foundry, TaleSpire symbiotes. _Winner: DDB Maps + Character Sheets in Maps_ on install base and momentum (free since Sept 2025; sheet sync 2026), with AboveVTT the fan favourite for depth. _Why it wins:_ players keep the sheet they already own; "point and click, super intuitive"; zero setup. _What it costs:_ the bridge is one-way and lossy (Beyond20 "only has access to the information displayed on the page"; Owlbear import is re-import; DDB condition sync "may need to refresh"); no product in this class does reach/OA/AoE/cover because the map does not know rules and the sheet does not know positions.

**C. Companion-only, theatre-of-the-mind (brain without a map).** Avrae, Shieldmaiden, Improved Initiative, Kobold+, DDB Encounters, PrismScroll/DDB app on phones next to a physical map. _Winner: Avrae_ for automation depth (attack→AC→damage→effects all resolved from data), _Shieldmaiden_ for DM ergonomics with live player view. _Why it wins:_ it runs on anything, costs nothing, and matches how most tables actually play (physical map or none). _Ceiling:_ everything positional is verbal — the DM says "he leaves your reach", then types `!i aoo`. This is where d20 Folio currently sits.

Cross-cutting evidence on taste: the market is bimodal. One camp wants maximal automation and accepts a maintenance tax (Foundry); the larger camp explicitly rejects "VTT as videogame" (Owlbear, DDB Maps, Alchemy narrative). Both camps agree on what the _sheet_ should own: conditions, effects, resources, concentration, HP.

## 4. Recommendation for d20 Folio (free, offline-first, phone-first, deterministic engine, not a VTT)

**Choose architecture C, upgraded with a minimal spatial model — "engagement graph + optional range bands" — and export/import to B-style maps rather than building one.** Reasoning:

1. _What OA automation actually consumes._ Gambit's/CPR detect OA by testing whether a mover exits the set of hostile tokens whose reach contains it. That predicate needs only _pairwise adjacency_ (is X within Y's reach: 5 ft / 10 ft) — not coordinates, not a grid, not fog. Theatre-of-the-mind DMs already track precisely this ("Engaged/Adjacent … Near … Far"; "only 10 feet and 30 feet matter in most combat encounters" — EN World Oct 2024; Sly Flourish's zones: "in danger of opportunity attacks if they 'move away' from enemies who've hit them with melee attacks"). An app that records **who is engaged with whom** can prompt the OA reaction deterministically ("Sheila leaves the Skeleton's reach — Skeleton may take an opportunity attack; Sentinel/PAM/Disengage/teleport rules applied") without a single pixel of map.
2. _What users demonstrably want from a map, ranked by the complaints above:_ HP visibility for the party (DDB Maps forum, Oct 2025), turn order everyone can see, conditions visible on the creature, "who is next to whom". Grid distance, LoS, cover and fog appear only in the Foundry camp's wish-lists. A companion can satisfy the first four with a roster/engagement view.
3. _Minimum map features actually needed for the automations in §2, in priority order:_
   - **Adjacency/engagement (pairwise, reach-aware: 5/10/15 ft)** → OA detection, nearby-foe disadvantage on ranged attacks, melee legality, grapple/shove eligibility, flanking if used. This is the one feature no companion offers and the whole OA story hinges on it.
   - **Range band per pair or per zone (engaged / near ≤30 / far ≤120 / remote)** → long-range disadvantage, out-of-range fail, spell range legality, which targets an AoE can plausibly include (Sly Flourish's "small area = 2 targets, medium = 3, large = 4" heuristic is the proven TotM abstraction).
   - **Explicit target sets for AoE** (DM/player picks from the roster, pre-filtered by band) → group saves, half/none damage, cantrip zero-on-save (Shard's exact model, which reviewers rate as sufficient).
   - **Not needed for automation:** absolute coordinates, grid, fog, lighting, cover geometry, movement-cost terrain. Cover can be a per-target toggle (half/three-quarters) the way Foundry's Token Cover ultimately reduces it to an active effect.
4. _Why not build B (a map) yourself:_ every thin-map product (DDB Maps, AboveVTT, Owlbear) took years to reach "HP number on token", and none crossed into rules-from-geometry; the value of a phone-first companion is that the physical table (or the DM's TV) remains the map. Offer **export of the encounter roster/state** to those maps (Owlbear GMG statblock format, DDB encounter, Foundry JSON via DDB-Importer-style import) instead of competing on canvas.
5. _Determinism and no-dice rule fit:_ engagement changes are user actions (deterministic, undoable), the OA prompt is a _prompt_, the attack roll stays with the user — exactly midi's "reaction dialog with timeout" pattern minus the RNG.

**Concrete minimum viable spatial model:** `Encounter.engagements: Set<pair(combatantId, combatantId, reachFt)>` + `Encounter.bands?: Map<pair, 'engaged'|'near'|'far'|'remote'>` + `Combatant.reach` (from weapons/size/features) + `Combatant.movementFlags` (disengaged, teleported, incapacitated). Reducer events: `engage`, `disengage`, `move(band)`, `teleport`. Rule: on `disengage/move` from a hostile whose reach contains the mover, and the mover did not Disengage/teleport and the hostile has its reaction and can see the mover → emit `reactionOpportunity(hostile, mover)`; feats (Sentinel, PAM, War Caster, Mobile) as access-policy grants over that event, mirroring Gambit's list.

## 5. UX evidence: how bridges surface reach/OA/reaction prompts to players

- **Foundry / midi-qol reaction dialog.** After an attack resolves hit/miss, midi "checks for reaction type items and shows those in a popup menu, letting the player choose which one to use"; the prompt goes to the _owner of the target token_ (or the GM for NPCs, per "GM Do Reactions"), lists eligible reaction activities (Shield, Hellish Rebuke, Absorb Elements…), and auto-dismisses after "Reaction Timeout" seconds (docs suggest 20–30 s) — "reaction dialogs will be removed if the reactee does not respond in time". Optional chat message mirrors the prompt. If the reaction was already spent, "Enforce Reactions" asks "do you want to continue?" rather than blocking. _Pattern:_ modal-with-timer, owner-scoped, chat fallback, soft enforcement.
- **Gambit's Premades OA / Counterspell.** Triggered by token movement out of a hostile's reach during combat ("only … while actors are in combat"); "Custom AppV2 Dialogs … with an integrated and animated countdown timer"; setting to show the dialog "to both the GM and Player. AFK player? No problem." — i.e. the GM can answer on behalf of an absent player. Feats change the _eligibility_ (PAM triggers on entering reach; Sentinel stops movement; Mobile suppresses). _Pattern:_ movement-triggered, time-boxed, GM override, feat-aware eligibility computed before the prompt. (Screenshots not obtainable: README states "All of the example videos below are now outdated" and carries no stills; the package page has none. See `refs4/foundry-gambits-premades.png`, `refs4/foundryhub-gambits.png` for the listing text.)
- **AC5e.** No prompt at all — it changes _which roll button is highlighted_ (advantage/disadvantage/normal) so the player sees the ruling before rolling. _Pattern:_ pre-roll affordance, not interruption. This is the lowest-friction reach/range UX observed and maps well to a phone.
- **Combat Range Overlay.** Reach as _colour on the map_: white ring = attackable now, blue = after one move, yellow = two, red = three+ (page text; images geo-blocked in capture, see `refs4/foundry-combat-range-overlay.png`). _Pattern:_ passive reach preview.
- **Shard.** Reactions are a filter on the consolidated actions block (action/bonus/reaction columns, `refs4/crops/shard-powers-2022.jpg`); AoE resolution is GM-side: select creatures → "roll their DEX saves … click them again, apply all their damage appropriately based on … individual saves". No prompt to players for reactions.
- **Beyond20.** No prompt; the DDB sheet gets a "B20" button, and the VTT chat receives a roll card (optionally whisper). Reach is invisible to it.
- **DDB Maps (2026).** Conditions applied from a token modal and mirrored to the sheet's Conditions section (`refs4/crops/ddb-condition-tracking-frame.png`: red-ringed selected token on the map; the GIF shows the condition modal); initiative panel with AC/HP/Init per creature (`refs4/crops/ddb-adjusting-hp-frame.png`). Nothing is prompted to players except "your turn".
- **Owlbear GMG.** Tracker rows with HP/AC/initiative + on-token HP labels (`refs4/crops/obr-gmg-main.png`); players get a limited "Player Action Window" for owned tokens. No reach/reaction affordances.
- **Roll20 2024.** Token actions open small "Macro Options" dialogs (`refs4/crops/roll20-automations-2.png`); Condition Sync marks the token when the sheet toggles a condition. No reaction prompts.
- **Avrae.** Text: the DM types `!i aoo Orc greataxe -t Sheila`; Avrae posts the resolved attack embed. The "prompt" is social, not software.

Takeaway for a phone companion: the two UX primitives worth stealing are AC5e's _pre-roll highlight_ (tell the player the ruling on the button they are about to press) and midi/Gambit's _owner-scoped, time-boxed reaction card with GM override and a chat/log fallback_. Nobody has combined them on a phone.
