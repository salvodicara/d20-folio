## 3. The rules surface and its honest classification (§4.2, §4.3)

The yardstick is the 256-item surface in
[2026-09-02-combat-rules-surface.md](2026-09-02-combat-rules-surface.md): 13 areas, every row with
its SRD 5.2.1 citation, the inputs the table must supply, what the engine derives, the entity scope

5.2.1 has no Lair Actions (2024 uses "in Lair" bonuses to Legendary uses/Resistance; `src/data/types.ts:2384`
already models `legendary.usesInLair`), and flanking was never an SRD rule. The licensing partition
holds: the 19 public feats are exactly the SRD 5.2.1 list; Heavy Armor Master, Vow of Enmity and
lair actions do not appear in public data.

The per-item classification (automated / modeled-not-automated / structurally impossible today /
table-only by design) with its evidence and root-cause aggregation lives in
[2026-09-02-combat-rules-classification.md](2026-09-02-combat-rules-classification.md). Headline
over the 256 items:

| Class                         | Items | Share |
| ----------------------------- | ----: | ----: |
| Automated                     |   123 |   48% |
| Modeled, not automated        |    87 |   34% |
| Structurally impossible today |    29 |   11% |

Self-side, single-character areas are genuinely automated (HP 20/23, conditions 13/20, attacks
