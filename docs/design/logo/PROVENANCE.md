# Folio logo — provenance

`folio-logo.svg` (full), `folio-logo-32.svg` (top bar) and `folio-logo-16.svg` (favicon) are original
artwork drawn for d20 Folio on 2026-10-26: hand-placed geometry on a 48-unit grid (regular hexagon,
central equilateral face, nine edges, a thin outer rim), gold gradient strokes, empty faces. No
third-party asset, no generated imagery; the owner holds all rights.

Chosen by the owner as version 3 "Inciso" among three line marks (Filo, Calligrafico, Inciso). It
replaces the faceted solid die (version B), chosen and then reversed by the owner the same day
(direction row 26).
