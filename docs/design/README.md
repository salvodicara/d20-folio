# Design · what is canonical

The approved design is the prototype in [`prototype/`](prototype/) (owner, 2026-11-02, direction rows 27–29). Every new screen copies it; nothing else in `docs/design/` overrides it.

**Ornament: «Lume»** (owner, 2026-11-03, replaces the prototype's filigree corners and the 3 px coloured header caps). One faint gold hairline (`--orn-line`), a lit top edge that fades at both ends (`--orn-lit`), a warm light from above (`--orn-light`) and a soft vignette below, on the hero, every panel and every dialog. A panel's colour is its header's light: a fading 1 px rule and a low tint. No corners anywhere. The prototype pages still draw the old frame; where they differ, Lume wins. The tokens and rules live in `src/ui/ui.css`.

**The rules the prototype was checked against** (all 102 pages × 1440, 1100, 768, 390, 320 px and 200% zoom, in Italian and English): no horizontal scroll, nothing clipped or cut with «…», no overlaps or text out of its box, no lonely last word, numbers kept with their units, text readable by real glyph height (≥ 12 px), AA contrast, 4/8 px grid, one panel-header component, touch targets ≥ 40 px, visible focus, every string translated, and none of the banned terms (product names, real people, internal codes, dev words, «!»). The design-system slice ports the same checks into the Playwright suite, so they guard the product too.
