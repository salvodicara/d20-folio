Find Familiar is now a real, playable companion. The spell card gains a "Summon
familiar" button (a wizard's spellbook or a Pact-of-the-Chain warlock's list) that
opens a form picker over every eligible form — every CR-0 Beast, plus the seven
Pact-of-the-Chain special forms (Imp, Pseudodragon, Quasit, …) — with the 2024
Celestial / Fey / Fiend type swap chosen right there (so an Imp-as-Fey reads "Fey",
never "Fiend (Devil)"). The summoned familiar lives in the Companions rail: its
form's stat block (numbers verbatim, type swapped), its current HP with ± steppers,
the pocket-dimension dismiss/recall, "one familiar only" re-forming, and — for a
Chain-Master warlock — the familiar's enhancements, finally on the sheet. The Find
Familiar spell text was re-sourced to the 2024 rules (eligible forms, the type swap,
Bonus-Action shared senses, touch delivery, pocket dimension). The form picker and
stat block load lazily, so a character without a familiar downloads zero bestiary
bytes.
