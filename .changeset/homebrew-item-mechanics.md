A homebrew weapon or item can declare its mechanics in the same shape as a catalogue magic item (grants while worn and attuned, typed charges with their dawn recovery and depletion, spells cast from those charges, attunement, rarity, type): they are stored unchanged on the character and in the library, and the item plays exactly like a catalogue one. A homebrew +1 weapon adds its bonus to its own row only, a homebrew wand spends and recovers its charges through the same controller, and a homebrew ring that needs attunement grants nothing until attuned. Homebrew grant sources now show their own name in cast-source lists. A guard test keeps every catalogue magic-item field expressible by a homebrew item.
