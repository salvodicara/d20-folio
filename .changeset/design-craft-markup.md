On the new sheet, «Danni» and «Cura» are now always an equal red and green pair, with their heart icons, also in the «Danni e cure» windows of the player and the DM. During a fight the sheet header no longer shows «Riposo breve», «Riposo lungo» and «Passa il tempo», which could not be used anyway; they come back when the fight ends. Screen readers get a heading order with no gaps, and texts that wait («Caricamento…», «Cerca…») use a real ellipsis.
