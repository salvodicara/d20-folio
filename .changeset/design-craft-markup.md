On the new sheet, «Danni» and «Cura» are now always an equal red and green pair, with their heart icons, also in the «Danni e cure» windows of the player and the DM. During a fight the sheet header no longer shows «Riposo breve», «Riposo lungo», «Passa il tempo» and «Sali di livello», which do not belong in a fight; they come back when it ends, and «Sali di livello» stays in the ⋯ menu. Screen readers get a heading order with no gaps, and texts that wait («Caricamento…», «Cerca…») use a real ellipsis.
