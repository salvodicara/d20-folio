Solo fights: when you end your turn while held by a spell or feature that lets you repeat the save (Hold Person's Paralyzed), a strip asks for the save, for example «Paralyzed (Hold Person): roll a Wisdom save to end it», on the classic sheet and on Play. «Saved: end it» removes the condition and «Failed: it stays» keeps it; each can be undone.
