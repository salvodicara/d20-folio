A structured homebrew item reaches the last catalogue item paths (pure core, no new screen): a homebrew weapon's charges resolve, gate (carried and attuned), recover at dawn and stop its grants once destroyed, under its own copy id; a homebrew item or weapon calls its creatures through the item summon groups, placed in the Initiative order as it declares; a homebrew potion's countdown and standing state arm when drunk (`potion:<instanceId>`); a homebrew weapon may declare its Weapon Mastery property (`mechanics.mastery`), which rides its row once a Weapon Mastery pick names that weapon. The homebrew spell's `mechanicsProgram` field is retired: no play path reads a spell's program, so a stored one stays verbatim and inert.
