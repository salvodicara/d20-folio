Brutal Strike follows its rule. Its extra die is offered only on a turn where you use Reckless Attack. Without it, the closed card shows no extra die, and the open card shows the die dimmed with «needs Reckless Attack». The text also states that the roll must not have Disadvantage, that only the most recent Hamstring Blow counts, and that an attack roll can gain only one Sundering Blow bonus.
