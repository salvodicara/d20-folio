A College of Lore Bard whose Magical Discoveries were saved as ordinary prepared spells now sees them named under «Choices to finish»: the two spells from the Cleric, Druid or Wizard list are preselected, and one Save keeps them always prepared and frees the Bard's normal prepared places they were taking. The player can pick different spells first, and the change can be undone. Nothing changes until the player confirms, and a Bard whose discoveries are already always prepared is never asked.
