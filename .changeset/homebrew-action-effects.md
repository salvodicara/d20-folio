A homebrew feature action can declare every effect a catalogue feature action has (damage on a save or a hit, a condition, a mark like Hunter's Mark, a held state, a check bonus, revival, a Hit Point threshold, …) in the catalogue action's own shape. Each field is checked on read and a field this version cannot read is left out of play, never erased. New generic riders, usable by catalogue entries, homebrew actions and homebrew monsters alike: a push or pull of N feet, a Speed reduction until a turn boundary, a Hit Point maximum drain (restored by a Long Rest) and a swallow (Blinded, Restrained, Total Cover, damage each turn), with English and Italian lines. Engine only: no editor yet.
