The wizards join the chrome reset — and a state no longer dissolves the plate it lights.

Creation and level-up were the screens the reset never reached: of ~250 `.wiz-*` rules exactly three
consumed a state token, the chosen class plaque wore its own selection frame, the hit-die chip kept
a border on a read-only classifier, and twenty-two rules emitted a bloom into a chrome that has
none. The whole family is on the one material now — the plate on both tiers, the state ladder for
hover/pressed/selected/disabled, eleven read-only facets and gems unframed, the 1px settle honoured
— and both wizards are in the framed-box census for the first time.

Fixes a live severe defect found on the way: `background-image` is a REPLACED property, so the
roster card's hover state was discarding the plate's own face and dome, and the tile went
translucent under the pointer with the backdrop art showing through it. The ladder now reaches a
plate through a veil slot that cannot strip it, and the guard derives its plate list from the
stylesheet instead of a list.
