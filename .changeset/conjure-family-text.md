Conjure Animals, Conjure Celestial, Conjure Elemental, Conjure Minor Elementals and Conjure Woodland Beings read the SRD 5.2.1 text in English and Italian (Animate Objects too, in Italian). The spell data follows: Conjure Animals and Conjure Elemental deal no damage on a successful save, Conjure Elemental scales 1d8 per slot level, and the Animals pack and the Woodland Beings emanation move with the caster instead of costing a Bonus Action.
