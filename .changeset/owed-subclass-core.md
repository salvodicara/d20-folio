A character past its subclass level with no subclass now owes it in «Scelte da completare» and in the choices-owed count. Picking a subclass, on the level-up or on the sheet, goes through one shared step: every subclass feature up to the class level, its spells, Armor Class and Hit Points, and the choices those features ask. A late pick on the level-up now asks the choices of the earlier subclass features too, and the subclass preview shows them.
