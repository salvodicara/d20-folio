Automate charged-item casts whose spell level changes with the number of charges spent. Magic Missile, Fireball, Lightning Bolt, Charm Person from Eyes of Charming, and Cure Wounds from Staff of Healing now offer only affordable item-defined levels, preview the scaled spell result before target selection, debit the exact charge cost in solo and encounter play, and restore that cost on undo.
