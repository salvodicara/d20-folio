Conditions (classic sheet and Play): a condition a spell lets you shake off with a save at the end of your turn, such as Paralyzed, still goes on with one tap as before. While it is on, a row under it asks «Paralyzed: from what?» [Hold Person] [Hold Monster] [Other]. Naming the spell is optional; then, in a solo fight, ending your turn asks for that save. «Other» names nothing. Frightened still asks «by whom?» as before. What you named is kept when you reload, and it goes when the condition goes.
