More of a monster's stat block is one tap for the DM: an attack's tray offers the conditions its hit gives (Grappled, Prone, Poisoned…; off when the print gates them on size or a charge, with the save when the hit forces one), and auras that just deal damage (Fire Aura, Corrosive Form, Barbed Hide) and moves that use another attack (a dragon's Pounce, Rampage, Riposte) open the same tray. Each fact is read from the stat block's own text and pinned to it by a test.
