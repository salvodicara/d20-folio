Ray of Enfeeblement now follows the 2024 rules: no attack roll, the target makes a Constitution save. On a failure it has Disadvantage on Strength checks, Strength saves and Strength-based attack rolls and subtracts 1d8 from its damage rolls for the spell's duration, and it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, a success ending the spell.
