Combat Chronicle — the DM tracker now writes the fight for you. As you book HP,
conditions and turns in the encounter, a factual live feed (the "Chronicle of the
fight") builds itself: each un-attributed hit offers a one-tap attacker picker
(pre-selected to the current combatant, always skippable, never auto-guessed), and the
active combatant gets a pull-only "log a miss / a pass". At "End encounter" an editable
entry — title, your free-text narrative, an inferred outcome, and the removable record
lines — saves ONE Chronicle chapter (the single write per fight) or skips.
