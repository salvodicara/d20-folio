A homebrew monster can carry the same structured stat block a bestiary monster has (attacks and saves with their hit conditions, Recharge and N/Day uses, legendary actions and resistance, spellcasting by tier, trait facts such as Regeneration and Magic Resistance, Multiattack, moves and breathing), written as structured entries or as plain stat-block text read by the same readers the SRD bestiary is checked against. It is stored unchanged in the library and runs through the same encounter counters, Multiattack plan, damage tray and Concentration. Engine only: no editor yet, and nothing links an encounter creature to it until the reader is live.
