Shared links now unfurl over a card that matches what you shared. A character link arrives on a
character card ("A character lives here"), a campaign invite on an invite card ("A seat at the
table"), and every other link on the d20 Folio card as before — three designed images in the same
folio hand, so the person you sent it to can tell what kind of link it is at a glance, before
reading a word. Links that describe nothing — unshared, locked, unknown — keep the plain app card,
exactly like any other page.
