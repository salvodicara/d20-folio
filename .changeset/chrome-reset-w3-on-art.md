Dark on-art text is readable — and the guard can finally see it.

The on-art legibility battery ran light-theme only, on the premise that "the art is dark, so light
ink is safe". Our backdrops have large bright regions: measured against the real composited pixels,
dark's own muted ink read 1.64:1 on the campaign hub's section counts, its gold rubrics 1.95:1 and
the treasury total chip 1.46:1. The dark halo that grounds loose ink is now one mechanism in both
themes, and a control sitting loose on the scene paints its own plate so its edge cannot dissolve
across a bright patch. The battery gained a contrast leg that runs in both themes and measures
against a screenshot of what the page actually paints.
