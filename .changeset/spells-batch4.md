Fourteen more spells carry their effect. Spare the Dying stabilizes the ally you pick. Slow gives its targets -2 AC and Dexterity saves and halves their Speed; Otto's Irresistible Dance, Holy Aura and Sanctuary light their Advantage and Disadvantage, and Sanctuary ends when the warded creature attacks, casts or deals damage. Banishment and Otto's offer their condition, Conjure Fey is a 3d12 + modifier Psychic attack that frightens, Conjure Minor Elementals adds 2d8 to your hits, and Power Word Kill shows its 12d12. Revivify, Raise Dead and the Resurrections, and the Power Words' Hit Point thresholds, are ready for the new damage tray.
