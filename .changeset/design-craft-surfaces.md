The new interface is calmer. Fewer boxes have a border: groups keep their bronze frame, and what sits inside them is set apart by a slightly lighter or darker surface instead of another outline. On Play, the turn strip is one flat band across the panel, with «Fine turno» and «Fine scontro» on its line. In a fight, the current turn's row is a flat band without the side bar. The glows around the campaign picture, the Hit Points number and the Armor Class shield are gone.
