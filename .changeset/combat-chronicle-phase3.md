Auto-narrated combat, Phase 3 — the Chronicle now reconciles saves, AoE and side-effects.
A declared area SAVE spell (Fireball class) fuses the DM's per-target HP drops into one
line: each damaged foe carries the DM's real number (half or full — always the truth,
never invented) and a target the DM left un-dropped is positively logged as having
resisted. A DM-booked condition is credited to the caster whose action RIDER applies it
(a Topple weapon's Prone), and an un-correlated condition stays a plain logged line. All
derived every render, never fabricated, and fully bilingual (EN + IT); the DM still
overrides or removes any line.
