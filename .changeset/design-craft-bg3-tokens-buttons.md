The new interface reads more cleanly. Every word and every number now uses one sturdy serif, so a number never mixes two fonts, and the numbers line up. The buttons are one family in Baldur's Gate 3's style: the blue lit button keeps its colour with a gold rim and clearer text, framed buttons have a brass edge, and every button now shows a clear hover, pressed, disabled and focus state. «Danni» and «Cura» are an equal red and green pair.
