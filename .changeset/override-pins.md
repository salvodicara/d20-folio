Every number can be set by hand: the engine reads and writes a new optional `build.pins` for effective ability scores, the save DC of any action (feature, maneuver, Grapple or Shove, Cunning Strike), carrying capacity and the jump distances. A pinned number keeps its computed lines plus one "Manual adjustment" line, and the sheet's numbers now include the ability scores, carrying capacity and both jumps (core only: no screen sets a pin yet).
