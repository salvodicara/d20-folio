More magic items carry their rules. Sword of Sharpness, Sword of Life Stealing and Mace of Smiting add their extra damage to a Critical row of the damage tray (Life Stealing never against a Construct or an Undead; Mace of Smiting 14 against a Construct). Adamantine Armor turns a Critical Hit against you into a normal hit, Mithral Armor drops Stealth Disadvantage and the Strength requirement, the Periapt of Wound Closure stabilizes you when a hit leaves you dying, and the Ring of Feather Falling slows a fall. The engine also knows the Periapt's doubled and the Potion of Vitality's maximized Hit Dice healing, the Oathbow's 3d6 against a sworn enemy, and what each Manual and Tome raises (+2, to a maximum of 30, kept after the book is gone); those wait for the Rest dialog and a Study action to read them.
