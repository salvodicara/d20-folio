The campaign hub stops framing what only groups.

Eleven of the hub's fourteen large framed boxes were non-interactive cards, and they nested — a
framed note row inside a framed section card. The sections now wear the app's grouping-rail material
(face, dome, grain, cast, no metal), their entries are frameless rows on the one hairline, and the
DM verb cells are frameless too. Sessions' hard `border-bottom` divider is the hairline like every
other separator in the app. Framed boxes: 41 → 37 on the hub and 52 → 48 in its running-encounter
view, nesting 3 → 2, with both census ceilings lowered to match.
