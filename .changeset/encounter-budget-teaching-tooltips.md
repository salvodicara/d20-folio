Explain-on-demand for the DM encounter-budget readout: the difficulty grade and the
costed-XP total now carry cockpit-grade teaching tooltips (the shared GlossaryTip
affordance, keyboard-reachable and touch-friendly, EN + IT). Tapping the grade teaches
the plain-language ladder (Low = easy win · Moderate = a real fight · High = dangerous
· Over High = likely deadly); tapping the total teaches what encounter XP is and how it
grades the fight. The chip's hover now threads the live costed XP into the budget math,
and the un-costed marker softens to "not yet graded" with a clearer hint.
