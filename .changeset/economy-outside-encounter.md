Outside a fight the turn economy only logs: two Bonus Actions (Second Wind, then a potion) or two Actions (Search, then Study) both apply with no End Turn in between, a reaction stays ready, and Sneak Attack's once-per-turn use is not spent. A fight starts when you roll Initiative, advance a round, or join a campaign encounter; there the per-turn limits hold as before.
