Every spell description and "Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot" line now reads the SRD 5.2.1 text in English and Italian (the ten spells built around a table keep their current text for now). Where the text showed the data was still 2014, the data follows it: Acid Splash is a 5-foot Sphere, Awaken has no size cap, Befuddlement and Glyph of Warding's Explosive Rune deal half damage on a success, Jump, Hideous Laughter, Spider Climb, Freedom of Movement and Gaseous Form gain a creature per higher slot, and Resurrection leaves the same fading −4 to D20 Tests as Raise Dead.
