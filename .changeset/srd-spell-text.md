Every spell description and "Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot" line now reads the SRD 5.2.1 text in English and Italian, including the ten spells built around a table (Augury, Confusion, Control Weather, Creation, Divine Word, Prismatic Spray, Prismatic Wall, Reincarnate, Scrying, Teleport), which now show their table rows. Where the text showed the data was still 2014, the data follows it: Acid Splash is a 5-foot Sphere, Awaken has no size cap, Befuddlement and Glyph of Warding's Explosive Rune deal half damage on a success, Jump, Hideous Laughter, Spider Climb, Freedom of Movement and Gaseous Form gain a creature per higher slot, and Resurrection leaves the same fading −4 to D20 Tests as Raise Dead.
