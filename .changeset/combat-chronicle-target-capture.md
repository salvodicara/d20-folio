Combat Chronicle Phase 1 (capture): when your sheet is in a live campaign encounter,
committing a weapon attack opens a compact target picker — pick the enemy you swung at,
then tap HIT or MISS after you roll. That target + outcome flows to the DM as the
deterministic record of your swing. Solo play is untouched: no target step, no hit/miss —
the capture UI only ever appears inside a live encounter, and nothing is written until you
tap a result.
