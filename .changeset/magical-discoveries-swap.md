A College of Lore Bard can now replace one Magical Discoveries spell each time they gain a Bard level from 7 on. The swap is optional and appears on both level-up screens. The new spell comes from the Cleric, Druid or Wizard list: a cantrip, or a spell of a level the Bard has slots for. It stays always prepared and does not count against prepared spells.
