A monster's Multiattack is now read from its stat block as a routine of its own attacks (two Rend attacks; a dragon swapping one for a spell; a captain's Scimitar and Pistol in any combination), ready for the DM to run in one go. Moves (Teleport 40 ft., Leap, Charge, Dash or Hide, Spider Climb) and breathing (Amphibious, Water Breathing) are read the same way, and the Vampire's Sunlight now shows its Disadvantage on attacks and checks.
