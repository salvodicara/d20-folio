The Combat Chronicle is now read from the session log and every member of the campaign sees it. "Who struck?" and striking a line are recorded as corrections (history is never deleted); a player can answer "Who struck?" for a hit their own character took. Monster HP totals stay hidden from players unless the monster is revealed or an ally. Saving a fight to the Chronicle also puts the DM's note and the outcome in the session report.
