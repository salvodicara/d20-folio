The new sheet levels a character up from the Play screen: «Sali di livello» opens Punti ferita (the average or your roll), Novità (only what the level asks, as chips, spells by level) and Riepilogo (Hit Points, proficiency bonus, slots and uses before → after, and what you gain). Confirming saves a snapshot first; «Annulla» on the toast puts it back. A new class, Spell Mastery and Signature Spells still open the classic wizard.
