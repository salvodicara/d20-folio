Berserker Frenzy follows its rule. Its extra dice are offered only while you rage and use Reckless Attack. Raging without Reckless Attack, the open card shows the dice dimmed with «needs Reckless Attack». The dice now take the type of the weapon or Unarmed Strike you use, so an Unarmed Strike's Frenzy is Bludgeoning, not Slashing.
