The chips in the new interface are one family in Baldur's Gate 3's style. A chip you can pick is a dark pill with a bronze line; the picked one lights with a pale-gold line and a warm glow, as BG3's selected tile. A state such as a condition or concentration is a small square-cornered tag in its own colour, smaller than any button, so the actions read first. The action filters are pills, and a fight row's tags («Ora», «Deve tirare») share one look. In the fight, a monster's actions are compact one-line chips with the name and the roll; the damage shows on hover and in the tray, and a use still available reads as ready, not as picked. Every chip is easier to hit, on a laptop and on a phone.
