The SRD bestiary's pushes, Speed losses, Hit Point maximum drains and swallows are now one-tap facts read from the stat block's own print: the bronze dragons' Repulsion Breath, the Fire Giant's Hammer Throw, the Frost Giant's Great Bow, the Steam Mephit's breath, the Wight's, Wraith's and Specter's Life Drain, the Vampire's bite (by the Necrotic damage taken), and the Behir's, Purple Worm's, Remorhaz's and Tarrasque's swallow, among others. A homebrew monster written as text gets the same facts from its print.
