Jumps are derived numbers with a breakdown: Long and High Jump from Strength (a Thief's Second-Story Work uses Dexterity when higher), plus a once-per-turn 30-ft leap row while Jump or the Boots of Striding and Springing hold. A Squeezing situation chip puts Disadvantage on your attacks and DEX saves and Advantage on attacks against you. Drinking a Potion of Heroism grants its 10 Temporary Hit Points, and a Potion of Speed that runs out leaves Haste's lethargy until the end of your next turn.
