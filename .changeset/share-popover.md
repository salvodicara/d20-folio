Sharing a character now lives in one small panel instead of two menu items. The sheet's ⋯ menu has a
single **Share** entry; it opens a popover with a switch — "Anyone with the link can view" — and,
while that is on, the link itself with Copy and (on phones) the native share sheet. Flipping the
switch off stops the share immediately; flipping it back on hands out the very same link. No
confirmation dialog, nothing to hunt for: the switch shows you the state and changes it. Handing out
a campaign invite opens the same panel, without the switch — an invite is a way in, not a visibility
setting.
