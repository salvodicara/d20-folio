**Randomize.** A tap on the new control keeps the class you chose — and its ability priority, so
the character stays playable — and draws everything else again: species and lineage, background
and its boosts, class skills, spells, languages, the Human origin feat, and every follow-up pick.
Tap again for another. It never touches a name you have typed, and the result is always a legal,
complete level 1 you can still edit by hand.
