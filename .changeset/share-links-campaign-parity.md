Sharing a campaign invite now works like sharing a character: one tap opens your phone's native
share sheet — WhatsApp, Telegram, Messages — with copy-link as the fallback everywhere else. The
campaign card's menu offered copy only before, so a DM on a phone could not hand the link straight
to a chat. The create-campaign success screen drops its raw read-only link field for the same
Copy / Share pair the campaign hub already shows, so an invite is handed out the same way wherever
you are.
