Custom monsters + monster portraits — the surfaces. The encounter Add-monster modal's
Custom tab is now a reusable LIBRARY: creating a monster saves it (name · type · CR ·
notes · art), and every saved monster is re-addable to any encounter from a searchable
list, with edit and delete. Monsters now carry ART beside the hero portraits: every
encounter combatant shows an uploaded portrait, or a legal creature-type glyph
(game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0) as the default, or the tinted monogram. A per-monster
portrait can be uploaded and cropped on the compendium/bestiary monster view (a per-user
override, remembered for every future add) and on a saved custom monster (kept with its
template) — reusing the same crop-and-upload flow as character portraits. The glyph
attribution is on the Legal & Attribution page.
