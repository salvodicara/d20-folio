Share a character from its sheet. The sheet's ⋯ menu gains **Share link** — one tap turns sharing on
and opens your phone's native share sheet (WhatsApp, Telegram, Messages…), or copies the link on
desktop — and, while a character is shared, **Stop sharing**, which asks first and then kills the
link immediately. Whoever you send it to needs no account: they open the link and read the sheet.
