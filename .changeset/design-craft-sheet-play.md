The character sheet reads cleanly at every size. In the Resources panel the counts line up and the «spend a slot» line wraps instead of being cut off. In the actions and spells tables the picture no longer touches the name, a long damage or range wraps neatly instead of running into the button, and a spell with both «Rituale» and «Lancia» shows them one under the other. On a phone, the ability scores, their saving throws, the AC, initiative and speed, and the item names are easier to tap, and a tapped row no longer stays highlighted. The rest dialogs list what you recover with small bullets.
