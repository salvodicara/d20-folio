Expertise follows the class table. A Bard or Rogue whose sheet holds fewer Expertise skills than its level gives (two at Bard 2 and two more at 9; two at Rogue 1 and two more at 6) is asked for the rest in «Choices to finish». This includes every Bard or Rogue made with the creation form, which asks no Expertise. The new creation screen asks for all of them (4 at Bard 9+, not 2).
