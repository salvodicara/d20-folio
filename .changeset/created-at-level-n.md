A character created at level 3 or 5 now matches one levelled up from 1: a Dwarf gains its +1 HP on every level-up, a Draconic Sorcerer gets its subclass HP for the levels already gained, a Paladin's level 2 gives only the Fighting Style the player picks (no extra Defense), and the ready-made build fills a caster's spells above level 1.
