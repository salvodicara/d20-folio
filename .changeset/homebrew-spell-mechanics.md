A homebrew spell can declare its mechanics in the same shape as a catalogue spell (save, attack, damage, area, targeting, effects lit on cast, a structured duration, ritual, upcast scaling, material cost): they are stored unchanged on the character and in the library, and the spell plays exactly like a catalogue one, with its save DC from the caster, its upcast dice, its buff lit on cast and its Concentration lapsing on the clock. A guard test keeps every catalogue spell field expressible by a homebrew spell.
