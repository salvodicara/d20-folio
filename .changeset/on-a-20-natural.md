The on-a-20 weapon damage (Sword of Sharpness, Sword of Life Stealing, Mace of Smiting) now needs a natural 20: a Critical Hit on a 19 adds nothing, and a Critical with no d20 face is offered to confirm instead of added. Damage of another type than the hit (Life Stealing's Necrotic) counts the target's own defense against that type, and Mace of Smiting gives an unknown target its 7.
