The level-up altar's caption stops ghosting — and the probe stops skipping the wizard.

Making the on-art treatment opt-in removed the surface exclusion list, but the wizard's region rule
kept no surface qualifier — so a caption standing on a PLATE inside the wizard column still took the
backdrop ink. In light theme the enthroned subclass altar's "Bonus spells · always prepared" was
cream on ivory at 1.04:1, and the level-up boon panel's head and count at 1.02:1: the same
cream-on-ivory the change existed to eliminate, one layer up. A plate grounds its own text now, so a
register standing on one keeps the ink it declares.

Nothing caught it because the deleted CSS exclusion had reappeared as a probe exclusion — the whole
wizard was skipped by the leak battery. It is walked like every other surface now, and both the
surface list and the register list are derived from the stylesheet's own materials rather than
maintained by hand.
