The ready-made picks now make the class's own choices too: Weapon Mastery (the starting kit's weapons first), a Fighting Style (Defense), Eldritch Invocations and Expertise (in the class skills), each still editable. The classic creation form never chooses them for you: a new sheet owes them, and «Choices to finish» asks them. A Bard created at 9 or a Rogue at 6 keeps all four Expertise skills, and a Champion created at 7 keeps both Fighting Styles.
