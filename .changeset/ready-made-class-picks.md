A new character no longer owes the class's own picks. The ready-made fill now makes Weapon Mastery (the starting kit's weapons first), a Fighting Style (Defense), Eldritch Invocations and Expertise (in the class skills), and the classic creation form stores them on «Create»; each stays editable. A Bard created at 9 or a Rogue at 6 keeps all four Expertise skills, and a Champion created at 7 keeps both Fighting Styles.
