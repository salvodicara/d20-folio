The engine counts a held breath (SRD Suffocation): 1 + Constitution modifier minutes of air, then 1 Exhaustion level at the end of each turn, all of them gone on breathing again; water breathing spares it. It also names the environment's Constitution saves (extreme cold and heat, frigid and deep water) and tracks a monster's Concentration with the save the DM rolls when it is hit. No visible change yet.
