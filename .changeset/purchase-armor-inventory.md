Buying and selling from the inventory. In Add Item, a catalogue item with a price shows "Buy for 75 gp" beside Add: one tap puts it in the inventory and pays from your coins, breaking larger coins and giving the change back (the toast shows every coin that moved), undoable and logged; when your coins fall short the button is off and says what they come to. An item card shows "Sell for 37 gp 5 sp", half its price as the SRD says, undoable and logged. Armor cards show the time to don and doff it, and equipping or removing armor says it; inside a fight it adds that it does not fit in one action, without blocking. Catalogue prices now show the Italian coin names (mo, ma, mr).
