A magic weapon's own extra damage now rides the weapon it is bound to, and only that weapon: Vicious Weapon, Sword of Wounding, Frost Brand, Thunderous Greatclub, Giant Slayer and Dragon Slayer (against their creature type), Mace of Disruption (against Fiends and Undead) and Flame Tongue while ablaze. A weapon with a rider but no +N can now be bound from the inventory too.
